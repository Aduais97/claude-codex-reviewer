## 10. OUTPUT STANDARDS AND METHODOLOGY

### 10.1 Severity taxonomy (apply consistently; calibration rules)
- **BLOCKER** — the package must not go to Franklin Law (or must go with the defect expressly
  flagged for repair) until fixed: broken cross-references that change legal effect; any fetter on
  regulator communication; any clause that defeats a stated deal_facts requirement; unresolved
  licensing preconditions to performance.
- **RECOMMENDED** — materially improves enforceability, workability, recourse or dispute-cost
  profile; should be adopted but transmission can proceed with the item on the instruction list
  to Franklin Law.
- **OPTIONAL** — drafting polish, belt-and-braces, or commercial preference; note and move on.
- Calibration rules: severity is set by **consequence if unfixed**, not by effort to fix. A
  one-word stale citation that redirects a permission to the wrong clause is a BLOCKER despite
  being a one-word fix. Where two severities are arguable, state both and choose with reasons.
  Operational items (Pass 6) take severity from their continuity consequence, drafting items from
  their legal consequence — do not down-rate an operational blocker because it needs no drafting.

### 10.2 Finding format (every finding, no exceptions)
> **[SEVERITY] [Doc — clause] One-line defect.**
> *Evidence:* quoted clause text (and deal_facts/research memo anchor where applicable).
> *Consequence if unfixed:* concrete scenario.
> *Fix:* exact replacement or insert wording, per document, ready to paste.
> *Interaction check:* other clauses the fix touches (re-run the Pass 3 table rows affected).

### 10.3 Evidence, citation and derived-metrics standard
- Every claim cites `document : clause` (e.g., `SPA 11.6`, `Advisory 4.1(a)`, `deal_facts —
  RESTRAINT RESCOPE`, `research memo §2`). No claim without a corpus anchor. Research memo is
  context, not deal fact — never cite it as if it were a term of the deal.
- **Derived-metrics rule:** arithmetic on corpus figures is permitted analysis and must show its
  formula inline (as in §§2.3B and 2.5); any metric requiring a non-corpus input is out of
  bounds. Benchmark cells without memo support are marked "not in corpus", never inferred.
- Quote clause language verbatim when the finding turns on wording; paraphrase is not evidence.
- Findings must be independently re-verifiable: a second reviewer with only the corpus must be
  able to confirm or refute each finding — and reproduce each derived metric — from its citations
  alone.

### 10.4 Verdict scales
- **Compliance verdict (Pass 4, per area and overall):** COMPLIANT / COMPLIANT WITH ACTIONS /
  NON-COMPLIANT / REFER TO SOLICITOR.
- **Recourse-adequacy verdict (Pass 5):** ADEQUATE / ADEQUATE WITH AMENDMENTS (every inadequacy
  has proposed wording or a framed commercial choice) / INADEQUATE (a material exposure has no
  proposed answer — escalate before transmission).
- **Closing-readiness verdict (Pass 6):** READY / READY WITH CONDITIONS (open items all have
  owners and dates inside the settlement timeline) / NOT READY (an unowned settlement-blocking
  item exists).
- **Benchmark row verdicts (Pass 7B):** market-consistent / tighter / looser / unusually exposed.
- **Final transmission verdict (whole package):**
  - **CLEAR TO TRANSMIT** — no BLOCKERs; RECOMMENDED items listed as instructions to Franklin Law.
  - **TRANSMIT WITH AMENDMENTS** — BLOCKERs exist but every one has ready-to-paste fix wording;
    apply the listed amendments first, then transmit without further review.
  - **HOLD** — at least one BLOCKER lacks a determinate fix (e.g., the CoA question if it cannot
    be resolved by drafting alone) or a deal_facts requirement is unimplemented in substance.
  - The verdict must name its conditions explicitly and assign each an owner (Purchaser action
    vs Franklin Law instruction vs PSPLA query), and must be consistent with the subsidiary
    verdicts (a package cannot be CLEAR with an INADEQUATE recourse verdict or a NOT READY
    closing verdict unexplained).

### 10.5 Working method for the report author
1. Read in the §0.3 order; extract the deal_facts requirement statements verbatim first — they
   are the acceptance criteria for Passes 1 and 2.
2. Build the §2 substrate before any pass: dashboard metrics (2.3B) with formulas shown, the
   transition map (2.4), and the valuation sanity check (2.5) — later passes cite these instead
   of recomputing.
3. Run Passes 1–7 **independently before** consolidating: a clause can pass its own pass and
   still fail an interaction (e.g., 11.5 passes Pass 2 but interacts with Advisory 1.2 in Pass 3;
   Advisory 9.1 passes a drafting read but fails the Pass 5F net-indemnity view).
4. For every stress test, argue the evader's side first, at full strength, then the drafting's
   answer — a boundary is verified only when the best evasion argument is defeated by clause text.
5. Populate the Pass 3 table exhaustively — all 21 template rows plus any reference discovered
   during reading; the table reports checked-and-resolved rows as affirmatively as broken ones.
   Apply the same affirmative-reporting rule to the Pass 6 checklist and the Pass 7B benchmark.
6. Disposition, don't just list: every warranty absence (5A), indemnity gap (5F), benchmark
   looseness (7B) and org-gap (2.4) ends in *justified* or *recommend a change* — no
   raised-and-dropped issues anywhere in the report.
7. Draft fix wording in the register and style of the host document (NZ plain-drafting; defined
   terms as used in that document; keep the SPA's clause architecture — do not renumber existing
   clauses in a fix if an interpolated number (12B, 4.5A) avoids a renumbering cascade like the
   one that broke Background B).
8. Consolidate into §9's risk register and §1's executive summary last; the transmission verdict
   is written only after every pass has closed and the subsidiary verdicts (compliance, recourse,
   readiness, benchmark) are reconciled with it.
9. Prior-round discipline: rounds 1–2 findings are recorded as applied — do not re-open them, but
   any fix proposed here must be checked for regression against their subject clauses.
