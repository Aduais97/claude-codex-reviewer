# ADVISORY AND CONSULTING AGREEMENT

ABSOLUTE SECURITY GROUP LIMITED (Company) x NIKKI WAIRAU CHRISTIAN (Advisor)

This Advisory and Consulting Agreement (the Agreement) is made as of [        ] 2026 between:

ABSOLUTE SECURITY GROUP LIMITED, company number 8517467, of 7/220 Queen Street, Auckland CBD 1010, New Zealand (the Company); and

NIKKI WAIRAU CHRISTIAN, an individual (the Advisor).


## Background

A.  The Company and the Advisor are parties to matters contemplated by clause 9.4 of the Agreement for Sale and Purchase of Shares between the Advisor, Ahmad Duais and Lockhart Trustee Services No.222 Limited (the Share Sale Agreement). This Agreement is the separate advisory agreement contemplated by that clause.

B.  The restraints, confidentiality and other obligations in the Share Sale Agreement continue to apply in accordance with their terms. Services performed under this Agreement are services provided directly to the Company within clause 10.4(c) of the Share Sale Agreement, and client contact in the course of services requested in writing under this Agreement is contact permitted by clause 11.5 of the Share Sale Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement varies the Share Sale Agreement.


## 1.  Engagement and Services

1.1  The Company engages the Advisor, and the Advisor agrees, to provide advisory and consulting services to the Company (the Services), being:

  (a)  quality-control operations and compliance checking of the Company’s teams — the core monthly focus of the engagement;

  (b)  maintaining and building the Company’s client relationships, and attending meetings with clients as a consultant where required by the Company;

  (c)  business development — identifying, introducing and assisting to secure new clients for the Company (clause 4 provides for commission); and

  (d)  such training and other consulting tasks as are agreed in writing under clause 3.3.

1.2  All Services are performed as directed or agreed in writing by Ahmad Duais on behalf of the Company. The Advisor shall not contact clients of the Company except in the course of Services requested in writing, consistent with clause 11.5 of the Share Sale Agreement.

1.3  Where the Company and SLS RTC Limited agree in writing, Services may extend to SLS RTC Limited on the terms of this Agreement.


## 2.  Hours and availability

2.1  The Advisor shall perform a minimum of six (6) hours of Services each calendar month, spread across a minimum of two (2) days in that month, at times agreed with the Company.

2.2  If the Company does not request, or does not require, Services in a month, the Advisor remains entitled to the monthly fee under clause 3.1 — the fee is a retainer for availability as much as for work performed.

2.3  If the Company has requested Services and the Advisor fails to perform the minimum hours under clause 2.1 in a month, the Company may withhold the monthly fee for that month. The Company shall give the Advisor written notice of the shortfall and, where practicable, a reasonable opportunity to make the hours up within the following ten (10) working days before withholding.


## 3.  Fees

3.1  The Company shall pay the Advisor a fee of NZ$1,500.00 plus GST (if any) per calendar month, payable monthly on invoice to Accounts@absolutesecuritygroup.co.nz, for the Services described in clause 1.1 up to the minimum hours in clause 2.1.

3.2  Time beyond the minimum hours is chargeable only where the additional hours and the task are agreed in writing in advance, at NZ$100.00 plus GST per hour, for training and other consulting tasks (clause 1.1(d)).

3.3  The Company will reimburse reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in providing the Services where approved in writing in advance and supported by receipts.


## 4.  New-client commission

4.1  A Qualifying New Client is a client which: (a) is first introduced to the Company by the Advisor during the term of this Agreement and is not, and has not in the twenty-four (24) months before introduction been, a client of the Company or of SLS RTC Limited; (b) enters into a written services agreement with the Company with a minimum contractual term of six (6) months; and (c) engages the Company for recurring contracted services — one-off events, casual or ad hoc bookings do not qualify.

4.2  For each Qualifying New Client, the Company shall pay the Advisor commission equal to thirteen percent (13%) of the Net Revenue received by the Company from that client in respect of services performed in the client’s first three (3) months of services (the Commission Quarter). Net Revenue means fees actually received by the Company, excluding GST, disbursements and pass-through costs.

4.3  Commission is paid in monthly instalments over the Commission Quarter: within fourteen (14) days after the end of each month of the Commission Quarter, the Company shall pay 13% of the Net Revenue received in that month, with a final wash-up within fourteen (14) days after the end of the Commission Quarter.

4.4  If the Qualifying New Client validly terminates within its minimum term for reasons attributable to the Advisor’s conduct, unpaid commission for that client ceases to accrue. Commission entitlements which have accrued under clause 4.2 before termination or expiry of this Agreement survive it.


## 5.  Term, renewal and termination

5.1  This Agreement commences on the Settlement Date under the Share Sale Agreement and runs for successive three (3) month terms. Each renewal is by mutual written agreement before the end of the current term.

5.2  Either party may terminate this Agreement by ninety (90) days’ written notice to the other.

5.3  The Company may terminate immediately by written notice if the Advisor materially breaches this Agreement or the Share Sale Agreement, or engages in conduct detrimental to the interests or reputation of the Company or SLS RTC Limited.

5.4  On termination the Advisor shall return all materials, credentials and Confidential Information of the Company, and clauses 4.4 (accrued commission), 6, 7, 8 and 10 survive termination.


## 6.  Confidentiality

6.1  The Advisor shall keep confidential all confidential and proprietary information of the Company and SLS RTC Limited received in the course of the Services, shall use it solely to perform the Services, and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law. This clause supplements, and does not limit or replace, clause 12 of the Share Sale Agreement (which is unlimited in time), and nothing in this Agreement or that clause prevents any communication with a regulator as provided in clause 14.4 of the Share Sale Agreement.


## 7.  Independent contractor; conflicts

7.1  The Advisor is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company. The Advisor has no authority to bind the Company or to represent the Company other than as an advisor acting within the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or employment relationship. The Advisor is responsible for the Advisor’s own tax and ACC obligations.

7.2  The Advisor shall promptly disclose to the Company any conflict of interest and work with the Company to resolve it. The restraints in clauses 10 and 11 of the Share Sale Agreement continue to apply and are not varied by this Agreement.


## 8.  Intellectual property

8.1  All work product and intellectual property created by the Advisor in the course of the Services is the sole property of the Company, and the Advisor assigns to the Company all rights in it.


## 9.  Indemnity

9.1  The Company shall indemnify the Advisor against claims arising out of the Advisor’s good faith performance of the Services, except in cases of gross negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of this Agreement or the Share Sale Agreement.


## 10.  Governing law and disputes

10.1  This Agreement is governed by New Zealand law. Any dispute shall first be the subject of good faith negotiation between the parties, then mediation in Auckland, and failing resolution shall be referred to arbitration on the terms of clause 17 of the Share Sale Agreement, which applies to this Agreement as if set out in full.
