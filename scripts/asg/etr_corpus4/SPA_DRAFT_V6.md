## BACKGROUND

A.  The Vendor is a shareholder of Absolute Security Group Limited (ASG). ASG is incorporated under company number 8517467 (NZBN 9429050952960) and has its registered office at 7/220 Queen Street, Auckland Central, Auckland 1010, New Zealand. ASG has 10,000 issued ordinary shares.

B.  The Vendor is a shareholder of SLS RTC Limited (SLS). SLS is incorporated under company number 9325956 (NZBN 9429052674921) and has its registered office at Office 2, Level 7, 220 Queen Street, Auckland Central, Auckland 1010, New Zealand. SLS has 10,000 issued ordinary shares.

C.  In this agreement ASG and SLS are together the Companies and each a Company.

D.  The Vendor holds 1 ordinary share in each Company personally, and 1,524 ordinary shares in each Company through Lockhart Trustee Services No.222 Limited (the Trustee) as the registered holder on the Vendor’s behalf. The Vendor has agreed to sell and the Purchaser has agreed to buy all of those shares, being 1,525 ordinary shares in ASG and 1,525 ordinary shares in SLS (together the Shares), together with all rights attaching to them. The Trustee is a party to this agreement (clause 1.2).

E.  The consideration for the sale reflects, in substantial part, the value to the Purchaser of the restraints and protections given by the Vendor in clauses 10 to 13, and the Vendor acknowledges that the Purchaser would not enter into this agreement without them.


## AGREEMENT


## 1.  Sale and purchase; interpretation

1.1  The Vendor shall sell and the Purchaser shall purchase the Shares, free of all mortgages, charges, liens, options and other encumbrances, with effect from 1 August 2026 or such other date agreed in writing by the parties (Settlement Date).

1.2  The Trustee is a party to this agreement as the registered holder of the Shares described in Background D. The Trustee shall transfer the Shares registered in its name in accordance with this agreement, and: (a) agrees to sell and transfer its Shares in accordance with this agreement and to perform clause 3 in respect of them; (b) warrants the matters in clauses 4.1(a) and 4.1(b) in respect of its Shares; and (c) covenants to comply with clauses 10 to 13 (other than any obligation which by its nature can only be performed by an individual) to the extent they are capable of applying to a body corporate, as if named as the Vendor.

1.3  In this agreement: working day means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a public holiday observed in Auckland, New Zealand; and a breach is material if its effect on either Company, its business or the value of the Shares is more than trivial.


## 2.  Purchase Price

2.1  The purchase price for the Shares is NZ$260,000.00 in total (Purchase Price), apportioned as to $[●] for the Shares in ASG and $[●] for the Shares in SLS.

2.2  The Purchase Price shall be satisfied by the Purchaser as follows:

  (a)  $100,000.00 on the Settlement Date;

  (b)  $80,000.00 on 1 September 2026; and

  (c)  $80,000.00 on 1 October 2026,

in each case by payment in cleared funds to the bank account nominated in writing by the Vendor at least two (2) working days before the relevant payment date.

2.3  The instalments under clauses 2.2(b) and 2.2(c) are subject to the Purchaser’s right of set-off in clause 15. Time is otherwise of the essence in respect of each payment.


## 3.  Settlement

3.1  Subject to receipt by the Vendor of the payment under clause 2.2(a), on the Settlement Date the Vendor shall deliver, or procure delivery, to the Purchaser of:

  (a)  transfers of the Shares in each Company to the Purchaser and/or the Purchaser’s nominee, duly executed by the relevant registered holder in registrable form;

  (b)  evidence of the passing of effective resolutions of the directors of each Company approving the transfers of the Shares and directing entry of the Purchaser and/or its nominee in each Company’s share register upon presentation of the executed transfers;

  (c)  unconditional releases of any charges (in registrable form where appropriate) over any of the Shares;

  (d)  if the Vendor holds office as a director or officer of either Company, the Vendor’s written resignation from each such office with effect from the Settlement Date;

  (e)  a written acknowledgement by the Vendor, in a form acceptable to the Purchaser, that neither Company has any liability to the Vendor (whether by way of fees, salary, current account, dividend, reimbursement of expenditure, compensation or otherwise) and that the Vendor has no claims of any nature whatsoever against either Company, except for the unpaid balance of the Purchase Price under this agreement and any final pay referred to in clause 9;

  (f)  all property of each Company in the Vendor’s possession or control, including any vehicles, equipment and other physical property, keys, access cards and credentials, uniforms, identification, devices, documents and records, client information, and all logins and administrative rights to email, software, banking and social media accounts of the Companies, domain name registrar, website and hosting accounts, and any records of intellectual property of either Company, together with the transfer or re-registration of any device, phone number or authentication method used for multi-factor access to any of those accounts;

  (g)  the share certificates (if any) for the Shares or, if none have been issued, written confirmation by each Company’s board to that effect;

  (h)  all forms and authorities required by each Company’s bank, accounting-software provider and payroll provider to remove the Vendor as a signatory or authorised user, duly executed by the Vendor;

  (i)  the record of settlement referred to in clause 9.2, signed by the Vendor; and

  (j)  waivers or consents, duly executed by every person entitled to them, in respect of any pre-emptive right, right of first refusal or other transfer restriction applying to the transfer of the Shares under either Company’s constitution or any shareholders’ agreement or, if no such rights or restrictions apply, written confirmation from each Company’s board to that effect.

3.2  From the Settlement Date the Vendor ceases to have any entitlement to dividends, distributions, drawings or other payments from either Company (other than the unpaid balance of the Purchase Price and any final pay referred to in clause 9), whether declared before or after the Settlement Date.

3.3  Payment under clause 2.2(a) and delivery under clause 3.1 shall take place contemporaneously on the Settlement Date and, if either party so requires, shall be effected through the parties’ solicitors against their undertakings. Neither party is obliged to complete unless the other completes at the same time, and neither party is in default under clauses 5, 6 or 7 by reason only of withholding its own performance while the other party’s contemporaneous performance remains outstanding.


## 4.  Warranties and pre-settlement conduct

4.1  The Vendor warrants to the Purchaser that, at the date of this agreement and at the Settlement Date:

  (a)  the Vendor is, directly and through the Trustee as registered holder under clause 1.2, the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Shares, free of all encumbrances, and no person has any option, right of first refusal or other right to acquire any of the Shares;

  (b)  the Vendor has full power and authority to enter into and perform this agreement, and, in respect of the Shares registered in the name of the Trustee, all trustee resolutions and authorisations required for the sale and transfer of those Shares have been obtained;

  (c)  the Vendor has not, and will not before the Settlement Date, do or permit anything which binds either Company outside the ordinary course of business, or withdraw, transfer or encumber any asset of either Company, other than with the prior written consent of the Purchaser;

  (d)  the Vendor has no undisclosed claims against either Company and is not aware of any liability of the Vendor to either Company which has not been disclosed to the Purchaser in writing; and

  (e)  the Vendor is responsible for the Vendor’s own tax (if any) arising from the sale of the Shares, and each party bears its own costs in connection with this agreement.

4.2  The warranties in clause 4.1 survive settlement.

4.3  From the date of this agreement until the Settlement Date, the Vendor shall not, except with the Purchaser’s prior written consent or in a form agreed under clause 13.2: (a) discuss the sale, or the future ownership or management of either Company, with any client, supplier or staff member of either Company; (b) copy, download, transmit or remove any client list or contact information, contract, pricing information, roster, or other record of either Company, other than as strictly required to perform the Vendor’s current duties; or (c) alter, delete, or change the access rights to, any account, system or record referred to in clause 3.1(f).

4.4  The Vendor warrants that as at the date of this agreement the Vendor has not: (a) solicited, diverted or encouraged away, or attempted to solicit, divert or encourage away, any client or staff member of either Company; or (b) copied, downloaded, transmitted or removed any record of the kind described in clause 4.3(b), except as required to perform the Vendor’s duties. On the Settlement Date the Vendor shall certify in writing that the Vendor has returned or permanently deleted all copies of all records of the kind described in clause 4.3(b) in the Vendor’s possession or control, other than records delivered under clause 3.1.

4.5  The Vendor acknowledges that the Vendor currently performs the client liaison role within ASG (the Vendor’s client-facing responsibilities being within ASG and not SLS). From the date of this agreement until the Settlement Date the Vendor shall act in complete good faith towards both Companies, shall use best endeavours to maintain and preserve the client relationships of both Companies, and shall promptly inform the Purchaser of any client dissatisfaction, complaint or risk of client loss of which the Vendor becomes aware.


## 5.  Interest on default

5.1  If any sum due by any party is not paid or satisfied by the due date (the other party not being in default under this agreement), the party in default shall, without prejudice to the rights of the non-defaulting party, pay interest at the rate of 12% per annum on the amount in default from the date of default until the default is remedied and all interest due under this clause has been paid.


## 6.  Purchaser default

6.1  If the Purchaser defaults in payment of any part of the Purchase Price, or in the performance of any other obligation under this agreement, and the default continues for fourteen (14) days after written notice from the Vendor requiring it to be remedied, the Vendor may, without prejudice to the Vendor’s other remedies at law or in equity:

  (a)  sue the Purchaser for the unpaid balance of the Purchase Price together with default interest under clause 5; or

  (b)  cancel this agreement and claim the Vendor’s losses by way of damages, in which case the Purchaser shall re-transfer the Shares to the Vendor against repayment of the amounts paid, less the Vendor’s reasonable costs and any damages recoverable.


## 7.  Vendor default

7.1  If the Vendor defaults in the performance of any of the Vendor’s obligations under this agreement then the Purchaser may, in the case of a default capable of remedy, after giving the Vendor not less than fourteen (14) days’ written notice requiring the default to be remedied and the default not having been remedied within that period, or in the case of a default not capable of remedy immediately, exercise all or any of the following rights without prejudice to any other rights, powers or remedies of the Purchaser:

  (a)  sue the Vendor for specific performance; or

  (b)  cancel this agreement and claim the Purchaser’s losses by way of damages.


## 8.  Release of guarantees; mutual releases

8.1  The Purchaser shall use the Purchaser’s best endeavours to procure the release of the Vendor from all guarantees, indemnities and other personal covenants given by the Vendor for the debts, liabilities or obligations of either Company, and pending release shall indemnify the Vendor against liability under them, except to the extent the liability arises from the Vendor’s own act, omission or default prior to the Settlement Date.

8.2  Subject to settlement occurring and to clause 4, with effect from the Settlement Date each party releases each other party from all claims arising out of or in connection with the Vendor’s shareholding in, and involvement in the management of, the Companies prior to the Settlement Date, except for (a) the obligations recorded in this agreement, (b) claims arising from fraud or dishonesty, and (c) any personal liability of a party to a regulator or other third party.


## 9.  Employment and engagement

9.1  Any employment or engagement of the Vendor by either Company ends on the Settlement Date. The relevant Company shall pay the Vendor’s final wages and accrued entitlements (including any entitlements under the Holidays Act 2003) in the normal pay cycle.

9.2  All employment-related matters between the Vendor and each Company shall be fully and finally settled by a record of settlement signed by the Vendor and the relevant Company and certified by a mediator under section 149 of the Employment Relations Act 2000. The parties shall sign the record of settlement on or before the Settlement Date and shall each use best endeavours to obtain certification at the earliest date a Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment mediator is available, whether before or promptly after the Settlement Date. The record of settlement shall relate to employment matters only and shall not restate or replace the restraints in clauses 10 to 13, which are given under this agreement in consideration of the Purchase Price.

9.3  For ten (10) working days after the Settlement Date, the Vendor shall, at the Purchaser’s reasonable request and without further payment, answer questions and provide information reasonably necessary to transfer the Vendor’s operational knowledge of the Companies (including in relation to rosters, client contacts, supplier arrangements and systems access). This clause does not require the Vendor to attend any workplace or perform any services.

9.4  The parties contemplate that, from settlement, the Vendor will provide advisory and consulting services to the Companies — principally quality-control operations and compliance checking of the Companies’ teams — under a separate written advisory agreement between the Vendor and the Companies. Any such services will be provided to the Companies alone; nothing in this agreement obliges the Vendor, the Purchaser or either Company to enter into that agreement, and its terms (including remuneration and termination) will be recorded in that agreement and not in this one. The ending of employment under clause 9.1, the record of settlement under clause 9.2, and the handover obligation under clause 9.3 are not affected by, and do not affect, any such advisory agreement, and clause 9.3 applies whether or not that agreement is entered into.


## 10.  Restraint of trade

10.1  In consideration of the Purchase Price, and to protect the goodwill of the Companies purchased under this agreement, the Vendor shall not, during the Restraint Period and within the Restraint Area, directly or indirectly, whether on the Vendor’s own account or as an employee, contractor, consultant, director, shareholder, partner, agent, financier or adviser of any other person or entity:

  (a)  carry on, be engaged in, or be concerned or interested in any business which provides security guarding, crowd control or host security, event security, mobile patrol, static guarding, retail loss prevention, personal or close protection, alarm or CCTV monitoring or response, cash-in-transit or valuables escort, concierge or corporate security, security training, the supply of security personnel (whether by employment agency, labour hire or subcontract), or security consultancy services (Restrained Business); or

  (b)  provide services of the kind provided by either Company to any client.

10.2  Restraint Period means twenty-four (24) months from the Settlement Date; if that period is held unenforceable, eighteen (18) months; if that period is held unenforceable, twelve (12) months; and if that period is held unenforceable, six (6) months.

10.3  Restraint Area means New Zealand; if that area is held unenforceable, the North Island of New Zealand; and if that area is held unenforceable, the Auckland region.

10.4  Clause 10.1 does not prevent the Vendor from (a) holding not more than 5% of any class of securities listed on a registered stock exchange; (b) working in a role which does not involve performing, managing, supervising, selling or soliciting any of the services described in clause 10.1(a), and which is not with or for a business that provides any of those services in competition with either Company; (c) providing services directly to either Company under a written agreement between the Vendor and that Company (including the advisory, quality-control and compliance services contemplated by clause 9.4), and not on behalf of, through, or in the course of any employment or engagement with, any other person or entity; or (d) any activity to which the Purchaser first consents in writing.

10.5  Each combination of the periods in clause 10.2 and areas in clause 10.3 constitutes a separate and severable restraint, and the parties agree the restraints are reasonable in scope, period and area, having regard to the Purchase Price and the Vendor’s knowledge of the Companies’ clients, staff, pricing and operations. The restraints in clauses 10 and 11 are given by the Vendor in the Vendor’s capacity as vendor of the Shares and in consideration of the Purchase Price, and not in the Vendor’s capacity as an employee of either Company; they are independent of, and survive the ending of, the employment and any engagement referred to in clause 9.


## 11.  Non-solicitation

11.1  The Vendor shall not, for twenty-four (24) months from the Settlement Date, in the Vendor’s own right or on behalf of any other person or entity, directly or indirectly:

  (a)  solicit, canvass, procure or otherwise be instrumental in the diversion of any client to any other business which provides a service that either Company provides, or accept the custom of any client in a Restrained Business;

  (b)  solicit, employ, engage or entice away any person who is, or was in the twelve (12) months before the Settlement Date, an employee or contractor of either Company, or encourage any such person to end their employment or engagement with either Company; or

  (c)  interfere with the relationship between either Company and any of its clients, suppliers, landlords or licensors.

11.2  In clauses 4.3 to 4.5, 10 and 11, client means any person to whom either Company provided services, or who engaged either Company to provide services (whether directly or through an agent, head contractor, facilities manager or venue operator, and including the operator or occupier of any site or venue at which either Company provided services), at any time in the twenty-four (24) months before the Settlement Date.

11.3  If the twenty-four (24) month period in clause 11.1 is held unenforceable, it shall be reduced to eighteen (18) months; if that period is held unenforceable, to twelve (12) months; and if that period is held unenforceable, to six (6) months. Each resulting restraint is separate and severable, and clause 10.5 applies to the restraints in this clause 11 as it applies to those in clause 10.

11.4  In this agreement, Client Protection Period means the period from the Settlement Date to and including 1 October 2026 (the date of the final instalment under clause 2.2(c)).

11.5  During the Client Protection Period the Vendor shall not contact or communicate with any client, directly or indirectly, in relation to the business of either Company or any Restrained Business, without the Purchaser’s prior written confirmation, except in the course of services requested in writing by a Company under the advisory agreement contemplated by clause 9.4. The Vendor shall continue to act in complete good faith towards both Companies during the Client Protection Period and shall do nothing which causes or contributes to any client ceasing to do business, or materially reducing its business, with either Company.

11.6  If, during the Client Protection Period, a client ceases to do business, or materially reduces its business, with either Company as a result of any act or omission directly contributed to by the Vendor in any capacity, that is a material breach of this clause 11 for the purposes of clause 15.1, and the loss the Purchaser may set off under clause 15 includes the loss reasonably estimated to arise from the cessation or reduction.


## 12.  Confidentiality

12.1  The Vendor shall keep confidential, and shall not use or disclose, any confidential information of either Company, including client lists and contacts, contract terms and pricing, rosters and staffing arrangements, financial information, and operational methods, except (a) as required by law, as expressly permitted by this agreement, or as made to any regulator in accordance with clause 14.4, (b) use or disclosure solely in the course of, and to the extent necessary for, providing services to either Company at that Company’s request (including under the advisory agreement contemplated by clause 9.4), or (c) information which is or becomes public other than through a breach of this clause. This obligation is not limited in time.

12.2  The parties shall keep the terms of this agreement confidential, except for disclosure to their professional advisers, banks and as required by law (including disclosure to the Companies Office, Inland Revenue, the Private Security Personnel Licensing Authority, or any other regulator).


## 13.  Non-disparagement and announcements

13.1  Neither party shall make any statement, publication or communication which disparages the other party, the Companies, or their officers, staff or services.

13.2  Communications to staff, clients and suppliers regarding the Vendor’s exit shall be made in a form agreed between the parties, both parties acting reasonably, and until agreed neither party shall make any announcement regarding the sale other than as required by law.


## 14.  Regulatory cooperation and communications

14.1  The Vendor shall, at the Purchaser’s reasonable request, cooperate with the Companies and their advisers in relation to any inquiry, investigation or proceeding by any regulator (including the Private Security Personnel Licensing Authority) concerning the period before the Settlement Date, including providing information and signing documents reasonably required. The Purchaser shall meet the Vendor’s reasonable out-of-pocket expenses of doing so.

14.2  Nothing in this agreement transfers to the Purchaser, or releases the Vendor from, any personal liability of the Vendor to any regulator arising from the Vendor’s own acts or omissions before the Settlement Date; and nothing in this agreement transfers to the Vendor any such liability of the Purchaser or the Companies.

14.3  Each party shall promptly make, and shall cooperate with the other in making, every notification, update or filing to the Private Security Personnel Licensing Authority, the Companies Office or any other regulator that is required as a result of the change in shareholding or officers of either Company effected by this agreement, and shall ensure that nothing in any such notification misstates or omits any material fact.

14.4  Nothing in this agreement (including clauses 4.3 to 4.5 and 10 to 13) prevents, restricts, penalises or discourages any party from making any report, complaint, disclosure or submission to, cooperating with, or otherwise communicating with, any regulator or government agency (including the Private Security Personnel Licensing Authority, its Complaints, Investigation and Prosecution Unit, the New Zealand Police, the Companies Office, Inland Revenue, WorkSafe New Zealand, or the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment), whether or not the communication is required by law, and no such communication is a breach of this agreement or grounds for set-off under clause 15.


## 15.  Set-off

15.1  If the Vendor is in material breach of clause 4.3, 4.4 or 4.5 or of clauses 10, 11, 12 or 13, or if any amount is due and payable by the Vendor to the Purchaser under clause 4 or clause 5, the Purchaser may, after giving the Vendor written notice with full particulars and seven (7) days to remedy the breach (where capable of remedy) or, where the breach is not capable of remedy, immediately upon giving that notice, withhold and set off against the instalments in clauses 2.2(b) and 2.2(c) the amount of the loss reasonably estimated by the Purchaser to arise from that breach or the amount so due. The notice must set out in reasonable detail the basis on which the Purchaser has estimated the loss.

15.2  Any amount withheld which an arbitrator (or a court) later determines was not properly set off shall be paid to the Vendor immediately, together with interest under clause 5 from the original due date. The Purchaser’s right of set-off does not limit any other remedy.

15.3  If the Purchaser withholds an amount in accordance with clause 15.1, that amount is not due, and the Purchaser is not in default, for the purposes of clauses 5 and 6 unless and until: (a) an arbitrator appointed under clause 17 (or a court) determines that the amount, or part of it, was not properly set off; and (b) the Purchaser fails to pay the amount so determined, together with interest under clause 15.2, within five (5) working days after that determination. The Vendor may not give a notice under clause 6.1, and may not cancel this agreement, in respect of an amount withheld in accordance with clause 15.1 except following a failure to pay under paragraph (b).


## 16.  General

16.1  Each party shall take all steps, sign all documents and exercise all rights, including voting rights, necessary to give effect to the matters set out in or contemplated by this agreement.

16.2  This agreement records the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and may be varied only in writing signed by each party. A waiver of any breach is not a waiver of any other breach. If any provision is held unenforceable it shall be severed or read down to the minimum extent necessary, and the remainder continues in force. This agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, and binds the parties’ personal representatives.

16.3  Notices under this agreement must be in writing and given by hand, by post or by email to the relevant party at the address stated below, or at any other address that party notifies in writing:

16.4  This agreement is governed by New Zealand law.

16.5  Clauses 1.3, 4, 5, 8, 12, 13, 14, 15.2, 15.3, 16 and 17 survive settlement and any cancellation of this agreement. Cancellation does not affect any right or obligation which has accrued before cancellation.


## 17.  Dispute resolution

17.1  If there is any dispute arising between the parties concerning the subject matter of this agreement or anything arising from this agreement or its construction, the parties shall endeavour to resolve the dispute as follows:

  (a)  the party initiating the dispute shall provide full written particulars of the dispute to the other party;

  (b)  the parties shall promptly meet and in good faith try to resolve the dispute using informal dispute resolution techniques, which may include negotiation, mediation, independent expert appraisal, or any other dispute resolution technique agreed by the parties; and

  (c)  if the dispute is not resolved within fourteen (14) days of the written particulars being given (or any longer period agreed by the parties), the dispute shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration Act 1996 before a single arbitrator agreed by the parties or, failing agreement, appointed by the President or a Vice-President for the time being of the New Zealand Law Society. Nothing in this clause prevents a party seeking urgent interim relief (including an injunction to enforce clauses 10 to 13) from the courts.
