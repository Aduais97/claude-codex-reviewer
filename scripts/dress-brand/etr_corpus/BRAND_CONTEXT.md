## NZ legal frame (established in this user's prior ETR-reviewed dupe-brand
## work — SŒURS fragrances, Jul 2026; same owner family)
- Fair Trading Act 1986: s9 (misleading conduct), s13 (false representations),
  s12A (unsubstantiated representations).
- Trade Marks Act 2002: s89 (infringement), s94/95 (comparative advertising
  limits). Using "Clio Peppiatt" in ads/listings to sell your dress is the
  danger zone.
- Copyright Act 1994: garment designs can be protected as industrially applied
  artistic works (~16-year term). A shared "bead theme"/aesthetic is fine;
  a stitch-for-stitch copy of a specific identifiable dress is not.
- Meta/TikTok ad policies restrict dupe claims INDEPENDENTLY of law —
  lawful-but-rejected is common.
- Prior verified production lesson (same family, SŒURS): AI-generated product
  video destroys fine detail (logotypes, and by extension beadwork) — real
  macro footage of hand-beading is both the safe path and the content moat.
