# WEEK-1 EXECUTION KIT

## 1. CONTENT HOOKS — 15 ready to film
Each hook = on-screen text + what to shoot. **(IH)** = only if beading is done
in-house and filmable; if not, use the QC variants and never claim
"hand-beaded by us".

### Process pillar (40% of output)
| # | Hook (on-screen text) | Shoot |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "3,000 beads. 11 hours. One dress." (use your real numbers) **(IH)** | Macro time-lapse of one panel being beaded, end on the finished dress. |
| 2 | "POV: you asked how the beads catch the light like that" **(IH)** | Bead tray → needle → fabric, close-up, slow. |
| 3 | "Which motif makes the drop? You're voting." **(IH)** | 3 motif samples side by side; ask comments to pick — comment bait is reach. |
| 4 | "The sound of a beaded dress" | ASMR: beads brushing, dress lifted, the swish. Works for both scenarios. |
| 5 | "Every dress gets this check before it ships" *(QC variant)* | Unboxing/inspection macro: bead-pull test, seam check, steam, repack. |
| 6 | "What's in every order: the spare-bead kit" *(QC variant)* | Packaging story — kit vial, styling card, numbered tag. |

### Try-on / styling pillar (30%)
| # | Hook | Shoot |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | "One dress. Three nights." | Same dress styled for a ball, a birthday dinner, race day. |
| 8 | "This dress is heavy — that's the point." | Hold it, let it drape; weight = beads = quality. Honest fit content sells. |
| 9 | "Will it survive a full night of dancing? We tested it." | Movement montage: walking, sitting, dancing, car exit. |
| 10 | "160cm vs 175cm — same dress, both real" | Two heights, no filter, hem position called out. |

### Drop / BTS pillar (20%)
| # | Hook | Shoot |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | "Only 30 of these will ever exist. Numbering them now." | Hand-writing tags #1–#30, lay them out. |
| 12 | "Why we'll never restock this motif" | Retired-motif story — scarcity said out loud, honestly. |
| 13 | "Drop 1: what sold out first, and what we learned" *(post-drop)* | Talking to camera over sell-through screen; builds Drop 2 demand. |

### Social proof pillar (10%)
| # | Hook | Shoot |
|---|---|---|
| 14 | "[Name] at [event] in #7 of 30" | Repost customer photo — screen the caption first (Guardrail 2: no designer names, no "dupe"). |
| 15 | "Photos you sent us this week" | Carousel of tagged event photos, permission asked in DMs. |

**Weekly floor (from the blueprint):** 5 TikToks, 3 Reels, 5 pins — never below.

## 2. OUTREACH TEMPLATES — copy, fill brackets, send

### A. Creator loan DM (IG/TikTok — NZ micro-creators 5k–50k)
"Hi [name] — I run [brand], a small NZ label making beaded event dresses in
numbered drops of 30. I saw you're going to [event] — I'd love to loan you a
dress for the night (we handle delivery + return, no cost). No script, your
honest take, just tag us and mark it #gifted as you normally would. If it's
your thing I'll send the lookbook — what size are you?"
*Rules: loan not gift (pool of 3–4 samples); gift only the best performer
after results; content rights + disclosure agreed in writing; never supply
comparison copy.*

### B. Listicle / affiliate pitch email
Subject: "NZ-made beaded event dresses — affiliate + reader bonus for your
roundups"
"Hi [name] — I'm the founder of [brand], an NZ label doing hand-finished
beaded occasion dresses in numbered 30-unit drops ([price range]). Readers of
your beaded-dress roundups would recognise the look immediately. We run
[10–15]% affiliate commission, and your readers get a code that adds our
spare-bead repair kit free (we don't discount — bonuses only). Photos, specs
and samples here: [link]. Happy to send a sample for shoot/review. Your words
entirely — we never supply comparison copy."
*Rule: their words, their disclosure. International pitches only once
international checkout is live.*

### C. Designer Wardrobe partnership inquiry
"Hi — I'm the founder of [brand], an NZ label making beaded occasion dresses
in small numbered drops. I'd like to discuss listing previous-drop pieces for
rental (consignment or lend) — current drops stay off-platform to protect
full-price buyers. Who's the right person to talk to about label
partnerships?"
*Listing copy follows Guardrail 1 — no designer names, no "dupe".*

### D. Stylist / photographer DM
"Hi [name] — [brand] here, NZ-made beaded event dresses. We keep a small
sample pool for shoots and editorial loans. If you've got an event, shoot or
client who needs the beaded look, it's yours for the week — credit/tag is all
we ask. Lookbook: [link]."

## 3. THE 7-DAY DROP RUNWAY
| Day | Action |
|---|---|

| D-5 | Numbering BTS (hook #11). "30 only" said plainly. |
| D-3 | Try-on + honest fit facts (weight, stretch, heights). |
| D-1 | Email the list: "The list shops at 7pm Thursday — 24h before we post it." |
| Drop | Thursday 7pm NZT: post + stories countdown + go live on the site. |
| D+1 | "X of 30 left" — only if true (Guardrail 4). |
| D+3 | Sold out → waitlist + retire-the-motif story. Not sold out → last-call, window closes Sunday. |
| D+5 | DM every buyer: event photo ask ("tag us, we repost"). Feed hook #15. |

## 4. SEO & LISTING BANK (name-clean)
- **Product title formula:** [Motif Name] Beaded Mini Dress — [Colour] | [Brand].
  Add "Hand-Beaded" only if substantiated for every unit.
- **Collections:** Beaded Mini Dresses NZ · Embellished Event Dresses · Ball
  Dresses NZ · Race Day Dresses NZ.
- **Image alt text:** "[colour] beaded mini dress with [motif] motif — NZ".
- **Hashtags:** #beadeddress #embellisheddress #eventdressnz #balldressnz
  #racedayoutfit #nzfashion #aucklandfashion (+ #handbeaded only if true).
  Never: designer names, #dupe, #inspiredby[designer].
- **Every product page:** garment weight · stretch · height-on-model · real
  dispatch/lead time · exchange policy "in addition to your Consumer
  Guarantees Act rights" · spare-bead kit bonus line.
