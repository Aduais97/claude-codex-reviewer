# South Corridor (Drury–Ōpaheke–Paerātā–Pukekohe–Karaka–Ramarama–Bombay) — Research, verified 1 Nov 2026

## VERDICT: MODERATE (leaning weak at ≤$1.5M in the core; moderate in the second ring)

The thesis has already substantially paid out: Drury East/West live-zoned (PC48 Kiwi Property Metro Centre, PC49 Fulton Hogan 184ha, PC50, PC51 Auranga operative), Drury + Paerātā rail stations OPENED 2 Aug 2026 (first new stations since 1972; Ngākōroa H1 2027; ~$450M programme), CRL running (opened 13 Sep 2026, ~10-min peak frequency Pukekohe–Drury), SH1 Papakura–Drury completing ~Aug 2030, Mill Rd revived as RoNS (fast-track lodged Sep 2026; $1.75B construction start late 2028 is an election pledge, not committed budget).

**Consequence: the catalyst zone is repriced past the budget.** Live-zoned Drury: 30 Burberry Rd 3.91ha MHU sold (mortgagee tender) Apr 2026 at $8.8M+GST ≈ **$2.25M/ha**; Drury FUZ special-purchaser datum $3.48M/ha (Health NZ hospital site). Minimum meaningful cheques $6M+. ≤$1.5M is priced out of every parcel where uplift is underwritten.

- Bombay: 1.42ha $990k (Paparata Rd)
- Pukekohe fringe: 5,800m² bare (Heights Rd, by negotiation)
- Karaka lifestyle belt: 2.27ha $2.75M / 4.16ha $3.15M — OVER budget and amenity-priced, not urbanisation-discounted

## Key facts
- Pukekohe-Paerātā Structure Plan (2019, 1,704ha, 14,000+ dwellings to 2048) delivering piecemeal by private plan change roughly yearly: PC95 operative Jan 2025, PC98 Jun 2025, PC110 Heights Rd FUZ→Light Industry Aug 2026, PC124 Gateway notified Jan 2026 in process, Golding Precinct 62.4ha MHU in operative AUP Jul 2026.
- FDS 2023: Drury among 6 first-decade investment priorities; Pukekohe-Paerātā NOT first-decade; parts of Ōpaheke-Drury FUZ REMOVED for flood risk. FDS to be revisited under the April 2026 Auckland Deal joint spatial plan.
- Development contributions: Drury priority area ≈ **$64k/dwelling** (2025/26 policy; draft was $84.9k; non-growth ~$20k) — "growth pays for growth" entrenched; $13.6B greenfield infrastructure bill flagged.
- Elite soils: NPS-HPL amendment keeps LUC1/2 protected; a "Special Agricultural Area" is explicitly proposed to keep Pukekohe protected — a 10-year hold on market-garden land could end still locked for horticulture.
- Flooding: council actively hostile to flood-exposed greenfield (appealed Sunfield's Mar 2026 fast-track over flood-plain risk; FDS de-zoned flood-prone FUZ).
- Competing supply: ~15,000ha FUZ region-wide + PC120 brownfield upzoning + Sunfield 3,850 homes approved + Drury-Ōpaheke 22,000 dwellings — oversupply of zoned land can suppress englobo uplift.
- Market tone (Bayleys Jun 2026): development-land market "shifting sideways"; buyers price deliverability (servicing, DCs, infra timing), not raw potential.

## Sub-area ranking for ≤$1.5M entry
1. **Ramarama / Drury South fringe** (Great South Rd–Ingram Rd–Ramarama interchange): 1–2ha bare $875k–$1.5M, wedged between near-sold-out 361ha Drury South Crossing (South Auckland ~950ha industrial shortfall), SH1 Ramarama interchange, Mill Rd planned southern terminus. Second-ring today, first-ring if FDS extends under Auckland Deal. Check LUC class parcel-by-parcel.
2. **Pukekohe east/north-east live-zoning frontier** (Golding Rd / Pukekohe East Rd / Heights Rd / Belmont edge): inside 2019 structure plan, PPCs going operative yearly, electrified rail + CRL. Highest probability of actual rezoning within 7–15 yrs at this budget — but smallest land quantum.
3. **Paerātā fringe** between Paerātā Rise (4,500 homes) and the new station: FUZ around a day-one rail station; occasional sub-$1.5M blocks (Burtt Rd / Paerātā Rd margins).
4. **Ōpaheke eastern edge** — only non-flood-plain terraces; parcel-level flood-viewer check is a hard gate.

## Avoid
- Drury Central/West/Karaka Rd corridor (repriced; $1.5M buys nothing)
- Karaka lifestyle belt (amenity premium, behind Drury West in sequencing)
- Flood-prone stream margins (Hingaia, Oira, Ngākōroa, Otūwairoa/Slippery Creek)
- Elite-soil LUC1/2 land around Pukekohe/Bombay (NPS-HPL Special Agricultural Area proposal)
- Bombay as an urbanisation bet (outside all structure plans)

Entry discipline: rate the corridor a buy only below ~$1M/ha on dry, non-elite-soil, non-flood land within ~2km of a live-zoned edge.
