# North Corridor (Albany–Paremoremo–Dairy Flat–Silverdale–Wainui) — Research, verified 1 Nov 2026

## VERDICT: MODERATE — strong direction, wrong decade for most of it

End-state not in doubt (~69,000 future residents planned Dairy Flat, designated 16km rapid transit corridor, two planned town centres, Planning Act 2026 passed 22 Sep 2026 abolishing rural-urban boundaries and forcing 30-year live zoning) — BUT Auckland Council's funded sequencing pushed Dairy Flat residential urbanisation from 2038 to **2050+**, Watercare calls servicing "more than 20 years away," Penlink slipped to 2029. The big Dairy Flat interior payoff sits BEYOND a 7–15 year hold (2033–2041 exit window), while FUZ land already trades at a ~750x premium to comparable rural land. Works at this horizon only at edges where zoning is moving now — Wainui/Milldale (fast-track approvals flowing) and Silverdale West industrial (107ha live-zoned via private plan change Oct 2025).

## Key facts (sources on file with research agent; all web-verified 1 Nov 2026)

**Sequencing / zoning**
- FDS 2023 (adopted Nov 2023) deferred Dairy Flat 2038 → 2050+; Silverdale/Ōrewa growth delayed "at least a decade"; ~800ha removed from FUZ region-wide. Timing slip is serial: FULSS 2017 implied ~2028+, 2021 position "late 2030s", FDS 2023 "2050+". (OurAuckland; Local Matters)
- Spatial Land Use Strategy adopted 2 Nov 2023: two new town centres sited ON the rapid transit corridor; pre-structure-plan route protection only, live zoning NOT programmed. (Auckland Council)
- PC103 (private, Fletcher/Fulton Hogan): 107ha Silverdale West FUZ → Light Industry; decision notified 30 Oct 2025 — first real live-zoning in corridor since Milldale, achieved AHEAD of council sequencing by private plan change; ~10,000-job precinct. (AC PC103; Cato Bolam; interest.co.nz)
- Milldale Stages 14–17 ("Milldale North", ~231ha, ~1,100 lots) approved under Fast-track Approvals Act 2026; Fulton Hogan building its own temporary wastewater plant; part of ~9,000-home Wainui programme with $91M IFF-funded trunk infrastructure. (fasttrack.govt.nz; Local Matters)
- COUNTER-MOVE Sep–Oct 2026: Council default = out-of-sequence developments get NO council infrastructure/services/funding; staff cited potential $5B bill; "growth pays for growth" policy Oct 2026; government confirming law change on development contributions for out-of-sequence projects. (OurAuckland; NZ Herald; RNZ)

**Infrastructure**
- Ō Mahurangi Penlink NOT open — delayed late 2026 → early 2028 → **2029** (Wēiti bridge problems). (Local Matters 7 Apr 2026; NZTA)
- Northern Busway ends at Albany (opened 2022); NO funded extension north. Rapid transit to Dairy Flat = designation only (Te Tupu Ngātahi NoR: 16km Albany→Milldale RTC route protection, 13 North NoRs lodged) — no funding or construction commitment. (NZTA; AC NoR recommendation)
- Watercare: Dairy Flat servicing "more than 20 years away"; Army Bay WWTP at capacity by 2027. (Local Matters; Watercare AR 2024-25)

**Government overlay**
- Going for Housing Growth (Jul 2024): abolish rural-urban boundaries; Tier 1 councils must live-zone 30 years of capacity at high growth projections. Planning Act 2026 + Natural Environment Act 2026 passed 22 Sep 2026; national policy direction due ~mid-2027. The pro-greenfield regime is LAW but its operative direction lands 2027+ — has NOT yet re-sequenced the North FUZ. Budget 2026: per-consented-home council incentive payments from 1 Apr 2027. (Beehive; HUD; Wikipedia Planning Act 2026)
- Feb 2026: Cabinet reduced Auckland intensification capacity target 2M → 1.6M (legislated ~1.4M Apr 2026) — marginally strengthens greenfield case. (1News; Greater Auckland)

**Pricing**
- Dairy Flat FUZ: 146 Top Rd CV $2.15M for 1ha; englobo benchmark avg ~$1.27M/ha (vs ~$1,700/ha comparable non-FUZ forest land ≈ 750x premium); FUZ holding rates to ~$27,500/yr; market softened since 2021-22, professional land-bankers topping up. (OneRoof Aug 2024)
- Non-FUZ rural Dairy Flat: 8.96ha bush block asking $770k+GST (~$86k/ha) — the FUZ premium is the product, not the dirt. (Bayleys 189428)
- Paremoremo/Albany Heights (Countryside Living, NOT FUZ): 2ha subdividable asking $2.049M (~$1.0M/ha); 2.17ha + house SOLD $2.85M Jun 2026; 16.8ha CL block pitched at land-bankers. (Bayleys; realestate.co.nz)
- Wainui/Milldale edge: rural-edge 1,797m² section asking $450k (~$250/m²); Milldale houses $1.29–1.37M; Wainui median $1.27M (+16.8% yr). (realestate.co.nz)

**Risks**
- Serial sequencing slips are the base case; wastewater is the binding constraint; flood plains through central Dairy Flat (Rangitōpuni catchment; Zone 3 geotech settlement, Waitematā Group soils); Ōkura legally dead for urbanisation (Environment Court 2018, protection final Jan 2019); council servicing boycott of out-of-sequence development; near-zero income yield on bare land.

## Sub-area ranking for ≤$1.5M, 7–15yr hold
1. **Wainui East / Milldale fringe** — only part of corridor where rezoning-to-titles is happening NOW; shortest path to uplift within window; $1.5M buys a meaningful edge parcel.
2. **Silverdale West / Dairy Flat industrial structure-plan area** (Wilks Rd–Pine Valley Rd–Dairy Flat Hwy triangle) — PC103 proved private plan changes work here ahead of sequence; industrial users self-fund servicing; adjacent FUZ remnants = highest-probability next conversions.
3. **Dairy Flat FUZ on the designated RTC alignment / future town-centre sites** (Bawden Rd–Dairy Flat Hwy) — deep-time bet only if bought ≤~$1.2–1.5M/ha, flood-checked; exit likely to a patient aggregator, not a developer.

**Moderate/speculative:** Paremoremo/Albany Heights Countryside Living — livable hold economics (~$1M/ha, Albany 5 min) but NOT FUZ, no sequencing signal; upside depends entirely on post-Planning-Act national direction (mid-2027+) opening non-FUZ fringe. Redvale similar + landfill blight, some Penlink/SH1 interchange optionality.

**Avoid:** Ōkura (court-settled no-go); flood-plain parcels central Dairy Flat; interior FUZ already priced ~$2M+/ha; hazard coastal pockets (Hatfields Beach removed from FUZ).

**Swing factor to watch 2027:** national policy direction under Planning Act 2026 (~mid-2027). If Housing Growth Targets force Auckland to live-zone 30 years of capacity, Dairy Flat's 2050+ sequencing becomes unenforceable and the corridor re-rates. Sep 2026 council servicing-boycott stance is the counterweight.
