# Brief for Tax Opinion: Land Taint and Purchase Structuring

Prepared 1 November 2026 for Ahmad Duais. Requested deliverable: a short written opinion before any offer is made. Offers are planned within two to four weeks.

## Background

Ahmad and family intend two possible Auckland property acquisitions in the near term, both long-term holds of seven years or more:

1. A pure land purchase of up to $1.5M (bare or near-bare land, Future Urban zoned, held for eventual rezoning uplift). Lead candidates: 100 Upper Orewa Road Wainui (2.46ha), 20 Follies Way Dairy Flat (2.0ha), 220 Kern Road Ramarama (2.14ha).
2. A separate residential investment purchase of up to $2M (inner Auckland, capital growth hold of five to seven years). Lead candidate: 32 Montrose Street Pt Chevalier (THAB zoned with an approved four-lot resource consent, which the buyer would hold, not implement).

Separately, Ahmad participates in a property development joint venture in Wanaka: 6 Cleland Close, a two-house development with total development cost of approximately $1.84M. Ahmad's precise legal role, ownership share, and the JV vehicle will be confirmed directly with you.

## Questions for opinion

1. **Business of development.** Does the Cleland Close JV amount to a business of erecting buildings or dealing in or developing land for the purposes of ITA 2007 ss CB 9 to CB 11, or is it a one-off undertaking falling short of a business? What facts would tip it either way?
2. **Association and the ten-year taint.** If the JV (or any JV entity) is such a business, is Ahmad an associated person under subpart YB, and would land acquired NOW by Ahmad, his wife, or a family trust be taxable on disposal within ten years of acquisition? Both intended holds (seven to fifteen years, and five to seven years) sit at least partly inside a ten-year window.
3. **Purpose or intention (CB 6).** The land-bank thesis is explicitly rezoning uplift with an eventual sale, likely to a developer. How should purchase intent be framed and documented (trust resolutions, finance applications, correspondence) so that a genuine long-term-hold position is protected, and where is the line beyond which CB 6 applies regardless of documentation?
4. **Vehicle.** Does acquiring through a new family trust (or other vehicle) with appropriate trustees change the association analysis or otherwise improve the tax and asset-protection position? Note an existing family trust is being restructured separately (Hamilton Locke acting); advise whether the purchase vehicle should be that trust, a new trust, or other.
5. **GST.** The land candidates are rural or lifestyle blocks, some marketed "plus GST (if any)". Advise on the correct GST position for each scenario: vendor registered versus not, whether the purchasing entity should register, compulsory zero-rating of land transactions between registered parties, and the risk of a $1.5M "plus GST" surprise breaching the budget cap.
6. **Bright-line and interaction.** Confirm the current bright-line treatment for each intended purchase and how an early forced sale (for example year five) would be taxed under each of the heads above.

## What we need

A concise written opinion (two to four pages is fine) answering the six questions, flagging any additional information you need from Ahmad, and stating clearly whether either intended purchase should be delayed or restructured before an offer is signed.
