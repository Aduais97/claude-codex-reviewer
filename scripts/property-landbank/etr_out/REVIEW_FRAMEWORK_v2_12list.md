## 2. METHODOLOGY PROTOCOLS (applied in every section)

### M1 — Claim classification (three orthogonal dimensions)
The prior single-axis classes were not operationally distinct (DRAFT-ONLY and ABSENT both described a draft claim without corpus support). Every number and claim in DRAFT_12LIST.md now receives one value on each dimension:

1. **Provenance** — where the claim appears:
   - `P-CORPUS(doc)` — appears in a supplied document other than the draft (cite document and passage).
   - `P-DRAFT` — appears only in DRAFT_12LIST.md.
2. **Corroboration** — whether anything else supports it:
   - `CORROBORATED` — independent in-corpus support exists (cite it).
   - `UNCORROBORATED` — no in-corpus support beyond the claim's own assertion.
   - `CONTRADICTED` — conflicts with in-corpus material (register at §S2.4; flagged, never adjudicated by invention).
3. **Inference validity** — for claims presented as derived or evidenced:
   - `STATED` — primary assertion, nothing derived.
   - `VALID-DERIVATION` — recomputable from cited inputs (show the arithmetic).
   - `INVALID-DERIVATION` — does not follow from its cited inputs (show why).
   - `ASSERTED-EVIDENCE` — the claim says a specific evidence artifact exists (photo, GIS query, sale record, audit log) but the artifact is not supplied; the claim OF evidence is itself unverified.

Severity attaches to combinations: a decision-bearing fact that is `P-DRAFT + UNCORROBORATED + ASSERTED-EVIDENCE` is the two-burns class. `P-DRAFT + UNCORROBORATED` listing facts are marked **"draft-only — no independent corroboration supplied in this corpus"** on every use.

### M2 — Arithmetic recomputation protocol
Every $/m² figure (stated or implied by tactics), every CV ratio, every staleness figure is recomputed from the draft's own area and price inputs (§S2.3 table). Area conversions explicit. An error that changes a decision number (rank, cap, band status) is BLOCKING; rounding noise is MINOR.

### M3 — Credibility-borrowing test
For each property: (a) extract its evidence-tier label (FUZ-GIS / FUZ-stated / CSL / RP); (b) extract every certainty-bearing word in its entry and any tier heading covering it; (c) test whether the wording claims more than the tier supports. Traps: Tier A headlined "the certainty tier" over 2050+-sequenced and parcel-unconfirmed members; preamble blanket claims ("all GIS point-checked", "NO candidate has grid-corridor hits at its checked point") vs per-entry hedges that contradict them (rank 7's point query hit the road reserve).

### M4 — Severity rubric and closure types
- **BLOCKING** — would mislead the family decision if the PDF ships as-is: overstated evidence tier (two-burns class); standing-rule violation or unresolved possible violation (§S7.2); band or defect risk unpriced on a profile; arithmetic error changing a decision number; eligibility (B2/B5) failure or unresolved eligibility on a shipping profile; a Hobson rejection the family meeting can break.
- **MAJOR** — material risk under-weighted or inconsistently applied: missing carry line (B10); misclassified or unscreened exit anchor; undisclosed date trap; defensible ranking inversion; unresolved cross-document conflict; missing listing identifier or photo sourcing for a shipping profile.
- **MINOR** — precision, wording, or formatting that changes no decision.
Tie-break: the two-burns history and unpriced-tail-risk intolerance push severity UP.

**Closure type (every finding carries one):**
- **WORDING-CLOSABLE** — fixable by the supplied replacement text.
- **EVIDENCE-CLOSABLE** — closes only when a named artifact is produced (title/DP search, parcel-level GIS capture, listing URL/ID + capture, rates assessment, land/improvements valuation split, rental appraisal, re-sweep documentation, photo set). **Wording cannot close these.** An explicit on-profile disclosure of the absence may reduce release risk only where disclosure is honest and sufficient; where the missing evidence determines eligibility (standing-rule identity, land-dominance, pylon status), disclosure does NOT suffice — the finding holds the affected profile at D3 (M6).

### M5 — Per-property verdict rules
- **KEEP** — no BLOCKING finding; corrections, if any, MINOR.
- **KEEP-WITH-CORRECTIONS** — inclusion and approximate rank defensible; all findings WORDING-CLOSABLE with the exact text supplied (cite controlling F#s).
- **RETAIN-CONDITIONAL** — inclusion defensible only if named EVIDENCE-CLOSABLE items close; the property stays in the list but its profile holds at D3 until the artifacts are produced.
- **REPLACE** — inclusion itself fails (standing-rule exposure, eligibility failure, in-band execution improbable, defect unpriceable). A REPLACE names the promoted reserve, and the promoted reserve must itself pass the §S3 band screen, §S4 defect screen, §S4-E eligibility screen, and §S11 asset screen **on corpus evidence**. Replacement QUALITY is the test. If no reserve qualifies, the verdict is CUT-WITHOUT-REPLACEMENT (anti-invention: never promote an unscreened reserve to keep the count at 12).
No count of verdicts carries gate weight by itself (M6).

### M6 — Gate rules (three separate decisions; residual-risk based)
The prior single gate conflated three approvals, allowed GO with unresolved MAJOR evidence failures, and used fixed replacement-count thresholds. Replaced:

- **D1 — Review-approach verdict:** does the method above actually cover B1–B11 against the actual draft? `SOUND / SOUND-WITH-GAPS(named) / UNSOUND`. Reviewer-designed procedure is approved here as procedure (§0.1), not asserted as client mandate.
- **D2 — Shortlist composition verdict:** aggregate the M5 verdicts and judge whether the retained-plus-promoted set remains the defensible best available **on the supplied evidence**. A count of REPLACE verdicts never decides this alone — three strong promotions can leave composition stronger; one irreplaceable top-rank failure can break it. D2 states explicitly whether the §3 structural gaps (undocumented band re-sweep, item 3; missing verification audit records, item 2) prevent affirming "the 12 BEST in band" as opposed to "12 individually defensible options."
- **D3 — Release readiness, per PDF:** `RELEASE / HOLD-UNTIL(named artifacts, owner, date) / DO-NOT-RELEASE`. Hard rules:
  1. No profile releases with an open EVIDENCE-CLOSABLE finding on eligibility — standing-rule identity (§S7.2), land-dominance (§S4-E), pylon/defect status that determines qualification.
  2. No profile releases without the §S11 asset checklist satisfied: correct listing URL/ID, verification stamp, attributable photos — or, for presentation-level items only, an explicit flagged absence.
  3. Missing carry disclosure (B10), unsupported or misclassified exit anchors (§S5.3), and undisclosed date traps (§S9) are material evidence/disclosure failures that must close — by evidence or honest on-profile disclosure per M4 — **before** release. They do not ride through under a conditional gate.
- **Overall gate — GO / CONDITIONAL_GO / NO_GO** — is a summary of **residual decision risk**: the risk that the family, reading the released pack, decides materially differently than they would on complete, correct information.
  - **GO** — D1 sound; D2 stable; every shipping profile at RELEASE.
  - **CONDITIONAL_GO** — D1 sound; D2 stable; ≥1 profile at HOLD-UNTIL with named artifacts, owners, and dates.
  - **NO_GO** — D1 unsound; or D2's top ranks indefensible on supplied evidence; or the pack's core claim ("the 12 best, verified") cannot be supported from available material regardless of wording.
  Replacement counts appear nowhere in this rule. (Vocabulary note: GO/CONDITIONAL_GO/NO_GO is the PARTIALLY SOURCED vocabulary per §0.1.)

### M7 — Date-ageing protocol
Every reliance with a shelf life carries its explicit date inline wherever used and registers once in §S9. "Verified active 1 Nov 2026" is a statement about 1 Nov 2026 only.

### M8 — Corpus-silence and evidence-degree wording rule (anti-invention, both directions)
- Silence is reported scope-limited: *"no supporting [benchmark / comp / appraisal / record] supplied in this corpus"* — never "no X exists for the corridor" or "no X exists anywhere."
- Degrees of support are preserved, not flattened: an unsupported in-corpus **estimate** (e.g., DRAFT_RECOMMENDATION.md's ~$650–750/wk rent estimate for the excluded 100 Upper Orewa) is cited as exactly that — neither ignored (an in-corpus figure exists) nor upgraded (it is not an independent rental appraisal and attaches to a different property).
- The review never asserts that unseen research, photographs, or sweeps occurred (§S6 Q6.5, §S7 Q7.1) — and never asserts they did not. Both are unresolved, with a reconciliation/production request.
