### F16 — MAJOR — Axis 6 (§7.4) [OUT-OF-CORPUS — verify with accountant/solicitor before offer]: GST analysis cannot precede the vehicle decision, and the zero-rating mechanics are absent
**Claim/section affected:** Universal Gate 2 ("model $1.5M as GST-inclusive worst case; confirm vendor's GST status") and Gate 4 (vehicle "settled before signing") — presented as parallel gates.

A passive land-bank hold is generally NOT a taxable activity; a grazing lease might support voluntary registration. So the GST outcome turns on the *buyer entity's* registration status and intended use — which is undecided (BUYER_CONTEXT: vehicle undecided, likely trust). Gate 2's "inclusive worst case" is directionally right for an unregistered buyer facing a registered vendor (+15% exposure on "plus GST (if any)" contracts, e.g. 109 Hunua per CANDIDATES.md), but the gates are mis-sequenced.
**Correction:** Reorder and reword: *"Gate 4 (vehicle + its GST registration position) MUST resolve before Gate 2 (GST modelling) — the vehicle decision determines whether zero-rating is available. Per candidate, record: vendor registered? buyer entity registered + taxable use? → zero-rated / plus-GST / no-GST. Do not sign any 'plus GST (if any)' contract before this is answered in writing by the accountant. Note: registering purely to zero-rate, while documenting a passive long-term hold for CB 6, creates tension an accountant must resolve — do not improvise this."*
