# HOTC Safety Team — Quarterly Report & Value Review (Quarter ending 31 July 2026; May–Jul 2026)
Prepared by Absolute Security Group for Heart of the City.

## KPIs
- 673 business calls responded to (the quarter). 667 incidents handled (the quarter).
- +52% calls in the June pilot vs baseline. $690k annual funding (was $800k).

## Executive summary
- Over May–July 2026 the team responded to 673 business calls and handled 667 recorded incidents, with continuous patrol coverage.
- Frontline output tracks funding. Programme has run on $690k (down from $800k) since a cut in April/May 2025; activity stepped down ~21% then and has held there apart from seasonal peaks.
- LCF pilot (one extra team, 2 Jun–24 Jul 2026) lifted calls +52% and roughly doubled incidents in June, then output fell back when it ended.
- Implication: precinct has demand for more coverage than the current budget funds; restoring/increasing funding would predictably increase safety output.

## The quarter (May–Jul 2026)
- May 180 calls / 169 incidents (post-cut baseline). June 265 / 271 (pilot peak, +47% calls / +60% incidents vs May). July 228 / 227 (pilot to 24 Jul; serious incidents 16, highest of quarter). Quarter total 673 calls / 667 incidents.
- June is the busiest month on record for incidents handled (271).

## The trend
- 2024 launch & ramp, peaking ~395 calls/month mid-2024 on the larger programme.
- Apr/May 2025 budget cut $800k→$690k: calls dropped ~21% (8.5→6.7/day) and settled lower, apart from Oct & Dec seasonal peaks.
- Jun–Jul 2026 LCF pilot: sharp temporary surge, then return to post-cut level.
- Two independent events (2025 cut, 2026 pilot) move output in step with funding.

## Value & return on funding
- $690k delivers ~2,500 business calls/yr + thousands of incidents + continuous patrol, at ~$276 per business call (core).
- Funding→output: cut −$110k(−14%) → calls −21%. Pilot +$41,150 → +136 calls & +136 incidents vs baseline.
- Pilot delivered extra output at ~$300 per extra call/incident — essentially the same rate as the core (~$276/call). Funding converts to output at a predictable, near-linear rate. Pilot output was additional, indicating unmet demand.
- Value beyond counts: deterrence, business confidence, keeping disorder from escalating to Police. (This measures business calls & recorded incidents, not every outcome.)
- Bottom line: a predictable, good-value safety investment; the cut-and-pilot evidence makes a data-backed case to protect and grow funding.
