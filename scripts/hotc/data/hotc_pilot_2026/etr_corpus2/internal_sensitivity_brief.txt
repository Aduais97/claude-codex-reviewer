CLIENT-READINESS SENSITIVITY BRIEF — what must NOT appear in the client-facing report

The report (REPORT.md) goes to the client (Heart of the City) and onward to Auckland Council funding
administrators. The following classes of INTERNAL ASG operational matters must not appear in it, and this
review must confirm their absence or flag any leakage, however indirect:

1. STAFFING FAILURES: guard no-shows, late arrivals, solo-guard periods caused by absence, guards not
   contactable, overtime disputes, shifts ending early, deployment delays caused by ASG (e.g. gear locked
   in an office, waiting for a second guard).
   Known internal incidents that occurred during the pilot and must NOT be in the report: second-guard
   no-show on 18 July (solo morning, SOP-restricted responses); afternoon deployment delayed ~45 min on
   6 June (gear locked upstairs); 7 June B-team guard absent (3 deployed instead of 4); night-guard late
   arrivals on 19/20 June; afternoon shifts ending early on 19/26 June and 3 July.

2. EQUIPMENT / ADMIN FAILURES: missing body-camera chargers, missing keys, Ops phone at low battery
   forcing standby periods, radios low battery, WhatsApp artifacts in logs.

3. INTERNAL REPORTING-QUALITY MECHANICS: duplicated or copy-pasted logs, stale dates, mislabelled shift
   blocks, missing team logs framed as a failure. NOTE the balance: the §13 data-sources paragraph must
   keep an honest provenance statement (that is a strength), but it should be phrased neutrally as a
   compilation method, not as an airing of internal reporting failures. Flag if the current §13 phrasing
   goes further than needed.

4. INTERNAL MANAGEMENT MATTERS: internal manager names or instructions (e.g. standby orders from
   management), internal HR/discipline, rostering decisions, anything that reads as ASG managing its own
   staffing problems.

5. THIRD-PARTY EXPOSURE: other ASG clients or sites must not be identifiable. The Viaduct static-guard
   references are acceptable only as coordination context, not as ASG servicing another client. Named
   internal staff must not appear (role titles are fine).

Also verify from the positive side: nothing in the report should blame or embarrass precinct businesses
(e.g. a store declining to release CCTV, a store failing to report a theft in a way that names them
negatively), and nothing should read as criticism of Police response times or Council.

The three capacity-affected instances in §4 (1 Jun queued caller, 6 Jun cafe call during an active
altercation, 2 Jul night concurrent calls) are APPROVED for inclusion by ASG's CEO — they are framed as
capacity findings, not staff failures. Do not flag these for removal; do check their framing stays
capacity-focused.
