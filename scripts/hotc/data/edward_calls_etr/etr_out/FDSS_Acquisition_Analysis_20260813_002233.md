9.2  LIKE-FOR-LIKE PER-RECORDED-DAY (basis-matched to the headline).
The prior memo's error was to pair a like-for-like VOLUME change (−11%) with
FULL-YEAR per-day rates (6.70 vs 8.09) that include the part-reported July 2025.
Basis-matched figures:
- Aug–Jun l-f-l per day:  6.76 (FY26) vs 7.94 (FY25) = −14.9%.
- Full year (incl. part-reported July): 6.70 vs 8.09 = −17.2%.
Key point: the per-day l-f-l decline (−14.9%) is LARGER than the volume decline
(−10.6%) because FY26 used MORE recorded days to produce FEWER calls. The headline
must present basis-matched metrics; the corrected headline is stated in §9.7.

9.3  VOLUME / COVERAGE / PRODUCTIVITY DECOMPOSITION of the −288-call l-f-l gap (Aug–Jun).
- Coverage (recorded-days) effect: (358 − 341) days × 7.94 (FY25 rate) = +135 calls.
- Productivity (per-day rate) effect: 358 days × (6.76 − 7.94) = −423 calls.
- Net: +135 − 423 = −288 ✓ (reconciles exactly to the observed gap).
Interpretation [S]: the ENTIRE like-for-like decline — and more — is a per-day
productivity fall. Recorded coverage did not shrink Aug–Jun; it GREW. This
quantitatively contradicts any "reduced coverage footprint" story (see §10).
