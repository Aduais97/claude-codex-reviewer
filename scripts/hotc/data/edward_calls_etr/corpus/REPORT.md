## Notes
- *July 2025 was part-reported (2 of ~4–5 weeks captured), so it is excluded from the year-on-year figure. The like-for-like comparison uses the 11 fully-reported months (Aug–Jun): 2,419 vs 2,707, −11%.
- Figures are total calls from businesses recorded in the weekly patrol data, summed by month. Detailed callout and incident breakdowns are available in the daily logs and weekly reports.
- FY25 operated a larger patrol-coverage programme than FY26; the year-on-year movement primarily reflects the reduced coverage footprint, not reduced business engagement per patrol delivered.
