Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, theft response, stolen item recovery, public assistance, altercation management, Police involvement, and Team 2 overtime due to a late incident.
Morning shift completed patrols across Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Customs Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral Church, Imperial Lane, Victoria Street, Elliott Street, Darby Street, High Street, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, Wilson Carpark, Fort Street, Kiwibank, Aotea Square, Auckland Library, and surrounding businesses. Morning shift included a Snickel Lane disturbance response, Look Sharp attempted theft where staff recovered the items before guard arrival, and a Lovisa theft where guards stopped the female POI outside Starbucks, recovered the items, issued a verbal trespass, and returned the items to store.
Team 2, consisting of Beni Mafi and Henry Hakaumotu, operated from 09:00 to 18:00, including overtime due to a late shoplifting incident. Team 2 completed welfare patrols across upper Queen Street, Federal Street, Upper Albert Street, Victoria Street, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Darby Street, Wellesley Street West, Myers Street, lower Queen Street, High Street, Vulcan Lane, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Fort Lane, Gore Street, St Patrick’s Cathedral, Albert Street, and Lower Albert Street. Team 2 assisted a member of the public with directions, separated two female vagrants involved in an altercation near the Civic bus station, assisted Team 1 with the Cotton On shoplifter who was later arrested by law enforcement at Waitematā Train Station, and handled a late incident involving multiple shoplifters where all items were recovered with a total value of $1,083.91.
Afternoon shift responded to the Cotton On theft, with the female POI pursued towards Commercial Bay and Waitematā Train Station. Law enforcement arrested the female on Platform 1 and stolen items were later returned to Cotton On. Guards also recovered stolen goods at Peter Alexander after a sensor alarm, moved Chuma along from Darby Street station underground, responded to a public urination call from Hasi, and moved a male from McDonald’s Queen Street. Later, guards witnessed an assault at the ANZ ATM, intervened, separated both individuals, called Police, and uploaded the matter to Auror.
Night shift completed Beta, Delta, Echo, and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Victoria Street, High Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Customs Street, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, Hobson Street, and surrounding areas. No major incidents were reported during night shift.
Overall, the key matters were the Cotton On theft and Police arrest at Waitematā Train Station, Team 2 multiple shoplifters incident with $1,083.91 recovered, Lovisa theft recovery and verbal trespass, Look Sharp attempted theft, Civic bus station altercation, Peter Alexander stolen goods recovery, ANZ ATM assault / Police called, and Team 2 overtime due to the late incident.
