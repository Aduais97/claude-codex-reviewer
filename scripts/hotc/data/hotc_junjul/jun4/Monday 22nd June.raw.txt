Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, vagrant move-ons, potential theft response, public nuisance management, Police involvement, and a building facility issue.
Morning shift completed Alpha, Charlie, Bravo, and Echo Routes across Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Queens Wharf, Customs Street, Albert Street, Imperial Lane, Federal Square, Cathedral Church, Aotea Square, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, High Street, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, Wilson Carpark, Fort Street, Kiwibank, and surrounding business areas. Key morning incidents included a trespassed individual inside McDonald’s Queen Street who became verbally aggressive before Police took over, and two street vagrants sleeping in front of Amelia Bakery and Cafe who were moved along without issue.
Team 2 was deployed from 09:00 to 17:00 and completed welfare patrols across lower Queen Street, Commercial Bay, High Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Tyler Street, Commerce Street, Hobson Street, Cathedral area, Victoria Street, Federal Street, Queen Street, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, and surrounding areas. Team 2 responded to Nike regarding a potential theft, stopped and searched the POI on lower Queen Street, and confirmed no stolen items were found. Team 2 completed the remainder of patrols without further incidents.
Afternoon shift responded to Community Patrol regarding Chuma refusing to move from Darby Street station underground, to Hasi regarding a male urinating in public, to McDonald’s Queen Street for a male move-on, and monitored the 220 Queen Street headquarters sliding door malfunction until the repair technician arrived. Guards also witnessed an assault at the ANZ ATM, intervened, separated both individuals, called Police, and uploaded the matter to Auror.
Night shift completed Beta, Delta, Echo, and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Victoria Street, High Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Customs Street, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, Hobson Street, and surrounding areas. No major incidents were reported during night shift.
Overall, the key matters were the McDonald’s Queen Street trespassed individual with Police attendance, Amelia Bakery and Cafe vagrant move-on, Nike potential theft / clear search, Chuma moved from Darby Street station, public urination near Localito, ANZ ATM assault / Police called, and the 220 Queen Street sliding door malfunction.
