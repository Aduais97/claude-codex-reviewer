Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, public reassurance, fire-related incidents, vagrant move-ons, suspicious behaviour checks, a verbal trespass, theft reporting, alcohol-related behaviour, Police attendance, and Team 2 support outside the core HOTC area.
Morning shift covered Queen Street, Aotea Square, Federal Street, Victoria Street, Fort Street, Gore Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, High Street, Chancery Square, and surrounding businesses. The main morning incident involved a mentally unstable male reported by Jo & Joe for banging on windows, later located at Queen Street and Shortland Street attempting to grab an e-scooter rider and approaching pedestrians. Guards de-escalated and monitored him until no further aggressive behaviour was observed. Morning shift also dealt with a rubbish bin fire outside Cotton On, moved along a female vagrant outside the old Dangerfield location, and issued a verbal trespass to vagrants at the same location following a request from Auckland Council Wardens.
Team 2, consisting of Beni and Mya, operated from 09:00 to 17:00 and covered upper Queen Street, lower Queen Street, Vulcan Lane, Customs Street, High Street, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Pocket Park, Fort Lane, Gore Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, and Lorne Street. Team 2 assisted a member of the public with directions, responded to Danny Doolans Bar for a male removal request where the male had already left, and later assisted Police with visible presence during a protest at Shed 10 near the Downtown Ferry Terminal.
Afternoon shift responded to Kiwiana for a suspicious male with a scooter, 125 Queen Street for a sleeping vagrant, Cotton On for a jacket theft, Fort Lane for a fire with Fire Service present, and multiple alcohol-related incidents. Guards also called Police after observing a male vagrant yelling aggressively at Asian members of the public and hitting properties/objects. Police attended quickly and arrested the male.
Night shift completed Beta, Delta, Echo, and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Victoria Street, High Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Customs Street, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, and surrounding areas. No major incidents were reported during night shift.
Overall, the key matters were the Jo & Joe / Queen Street mentally unstable male incident, Cotton On rubbish bin fire, Old Dangerfield verbal trespass, Danny Doolans male removal request, Shed 10 protest / Police support, Cotton On jacket theft, Fort Lane fire / Fire Service attendance, aggressive vagrant arrested by Police, and alcohol-related public nuisance incidents.
