Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, weather-adjusted patrols, vagrant move-ons, under-influence welfare checks, public nuisance responses, and business assistance.
Morning shift completed Alpha and Delta Routes across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Elliott Street, Victoria Street, Commercial Bay, Fort Lane, Fort Street, High Street, Durham Street East, Canterbury Arcade, Chancery Square, Lower Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Aotea Gifts, Metro Woolworths, Atrium on Elliott, Movenpick, and surrounding stores. Heavy rain and strong winds affected patrols, requiring guards to use sheltered areas where needed. A Movenpick response was received for an individual refusing to leave, but the matter was stood down after the POI left. A Typo response involved a male who appeared under the influence and had difficulty maintaining balance; guards completed a welfare check and advised that Council should request ambulance assistance if required.
Team 2 operated from 09:30 to 17:00, covering upper Queen Street, Aotea Square, Lorne Street, Victoria Street, Federal Street, Cathedral area, Auckland Library, lower Queen Street, Commercial Bay, and surrounding areas. No major incidents occurred during Team 2 patrols. Team 2 was dispatched to the Movenpick call, but the response was cancelled after the POI had already left.
Afternoon shift responded to multiple business assistance calls, including vagrants at Celine’s Darby Street, a female playing loud music outside Ippondo, a male under the influence at Queens Arcade who left before arrival, suspicious persons in Wilson Carpark, a male under the influence at Canterbury Arcade, an aggressive male at Surf n Turf Backpackers, and a male drinking/vaping at Jo & Joe. All matters were resolved without escalation.
Night shift completed Alpha and Beta Routes across Commercial Bay, Britomart, Aotea Square, Bar101, McDonald’s Queen Street, Victoria Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Auckland Harbour Suites, Lorne Street, Wyndham Street, and surrounding areas. The main night matter involved a male sleeping inside McDonald’s Queen Street. Guards attended, the individual bought food, remained calm, and staff advised the matter was resolved.
Overall, the key matters were the Movenpick Hotel nuisance POI stand-down, Typo under-influence welfare concern, Celine’s vagrant move-on, Ippondo loud music / loitering female, Canterbury Arcade under-influence move-on, Surf n Turf Backpackers aggressive male, Jo & Joe public drinking/vaping incident, McDonald’s Queen Street sleeping individual, and the weather / missing charger equipment issue.
