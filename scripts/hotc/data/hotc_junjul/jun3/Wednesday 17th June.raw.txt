Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, visible security presence, vagrant move-ons, disorderly behaviour response, Police attendance, trespass action, public nuisance management, and equipment issues.
Morning shift commenced with Alpha Route and covered Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Customs Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral Church, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Chancery Square, O’Connell Lane, Courthouse Lane, Vulcan Lane, Wilson Carpark, Fort Street, Kiwibank, and surrounding areas. The most significant morning matter occurred at McDonald’s Britomart, where a male was sleeping inside the men’s restroom, became verbally aggressive, consumed alcohol, and vaped inside the premises. Police attended and the POI was trespassed. Additional morning matters included a group of vagrants moved on from Metropolis Hotel, a suspicious male reported at Commercial Bay, and Junior being checked after sitting on a power box outside Mecca.
Team 2 completed patrols from 09:20 to 17:00 across upper Queen Street, Aotea Square, Elliott Street, Victoria Street, Federal Street, lower Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Gore Street, Fort Street, High Street, Chancery Square, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, and surrounding areas. Team 2 had one incident at Ippondo where a vagrant was loitering outside the store. The individual was moved along without issue and the matter was reported to Auror.
Afternoon shift responded to Peter Alexander for an intoxicated male who had fallen and tipped shelves over inside the store. Guards retrieved store property from the POI, verbally trespassed him, and posted the matter to Auror. Patrols continued across Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Customs Street, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, High Street, Chancery Square, Lorne Street, Aotea Square, and Victoria Street. Celine’s Darby Street also reported a female yelling outside while drinking in public; she was moved along without issue.
Night shift responded to McDonald’s Queen Street regarding a verbally aggressive male sleeping inside the premises. Guards moved him outside and Police intervened. QF Tavern also reported a large fight inside the bar. By the time guards arrived, the fight had ended and all parties had left. Footage was obtained from staff. The remainder of the night shift patrols across Commercial Bay, Britomart, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Wyndham Street, and surrounding areas were completed without further major issues.
Overall, the key matters were the McDonald’s Britomart restroom incident with Police attendance and trespass, Peter Alexander intoxicated male / verbal trespass, McDonald’s Queen Street aggressive male with Police intervention, QF Tavern fight, Ippondo vagrant loitering incident, and the missing keys / missing body camera chargers equipment issue.
