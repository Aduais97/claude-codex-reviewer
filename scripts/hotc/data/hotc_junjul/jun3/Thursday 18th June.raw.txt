Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, visible security presence, wet weather patrol adjustments, vagrant move-ons, suspicious behaviour checks, alleged theft incidents, verbal trespass actions, public nuisance response, and equipment issues.
Morning shift completed Alpha and Echo Routes across Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Customs Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral Church, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, High Street, Vulcan Lane, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Kiwibank, and surrounding areas. Key morning matters included the 220 Queen Street / Data Centre complaint regarding a POI defecating on the rear stairs, a suspicious individual at Sky Mart Convenience Store, suspicious persons in a red BMW outside Jewellers Workshop, vagrants harassing customers at McDonald’s Britomart, and a group of vagrants obstructing Ippondo.
Team 2 operated from 09:00 to 17:00 and covered upper Queen Street, Aotea Square, Elliott Street, Victoria Street, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, lower Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Tyler Street, Commerce Street, Gore Street, Fort Street, High Street, Freyberg Square, and Chancery Square. Team 2 handled the Starbucks Queen Street incident involving a trespassed female who allegedly took toiletries, used racist language, and hit into a female customer while leaving. Team 2 also responded to Ippondo for a loitering vagrant and Mecca for a welfare check involving an unstable vagrant yelling at members of the public.
Afternoon shift responded to multiple incidents, including a follow-up at Ippondo regarding an individual with an auto bike, a male causing disturbance outside headquarters, Daiso Japan theft, QF Tavern aggressive male refusal to leave, Starbucks vagrant inside store, and McDonald’s Queen Street harassment. The QF Tavern POI was removed, verbally trespassed, recorded on bodycam, and the incident was posted to Auror. The HQ office vagrant was also calmly removed and verbally trespassed.
Night shift completed Alpha, Beta, Delta, and Echo Routes across Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Britomart, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Victoria Street, High Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Auckland Harbour Suites, Lorne Street, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, and surrounding areas. No major incidents were reported during night shift.
Overall, the key matters were the Starbucks Queen Street trespassed female / alleged toiletries theft / racial abuse incident, QF Tavern aggressive male verbal trespass, HQ unannounced street vagrant verbal trespass, Daiso Japan theft, Ippondo vagrant obstruction, 220 Queen Street / Data Centre defecating POI, and the missing keys / missing body camera chargers equipment issue.
