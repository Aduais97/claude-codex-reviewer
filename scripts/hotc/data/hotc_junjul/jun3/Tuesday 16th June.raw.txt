Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, theft response, physical altercation monitoring, vagrant move-ons, public nuisance response, Police notification, and equipment issues.
Morning shift completed Route Charlie, Echo Route, and patrols across Queen Street, Wyndham Street, Shortland Street, Imperial Lane, McDonald’s Britomart, Commercial Bay, Customs Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral Church, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, High Street, Chancery Square, O’Connell Lane, Vulcan Lane, Wilson Carpark, Fort Street, Commerce Street, Quay Street, and surrounding businesses. Notable morning matters included a male sleeping inside McDonald’s Britomart, a Team 2 report of female vagrants assaulting another female, a management report of monks allegedly scamming people for money, a verbal argument outside Michael Hill, and the Albert Mart Convenience Store theft of a pack of Rothmans 20s.
Team 2, consisting of Beni and Henry, operated from 09:00 to 17:00 across the upper and lower CBD. They observed a physical altercation near Aotea Square / NZ School of Tourism that de-escalated without intervention, attended Michael Hill after a report of vagrants fighting, covered upper and lower CBD welfare routes, moved vagrants away from Jo & Jo, and responded to a High Street / Lorne Street shoplifting assistance request where the POI had escaped before arrival.
Afternoon shift responded to multiple theft-related incidents, including Dangerfield, Brand Warehouse, and Whimsical Blu. The Whimsical Blu stolen items were successfully recovered from the female POI and returned to the store. Brand Warehouse had multiple theft-related reports, including two suspected males who were checked with no items found, and another male who fled with stolen items. Guards received photos and footage, and an Auror report is to be completed. Afternoon patrols also included Aotea Square, Victoria Street, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, Commercial Bay, Gore Street, Fort Street, Chancery Square, and Freyberg Square. A female yelling and drinking in public outside Celine’s was moved along without further issue.
Night shift completed patrols across Chemist Warehouse / Kathmandu, Vulcan Lane, McDonald’s Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Aotea Square, McDonald’s Queen Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Kitchener Street, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Gore Street, St Patrick’s Cathedral, Victoria Street, Wyndham Street, Elliott Street, 77 Convenience Store, and Sky Mart Convenience Store. No major incidents were reported during night shift.
Overall, the key matters were the Albert Mart Convenience Store theft, Dangerfield theft, multiple Brand Warehouse theft reports, Whimsical Blu theft recovery, Team 2 physical altercation observation near Aotea Square, Michael Hill altercation report, Jo & Jo vagrant move-on, Celine’s public drinking/yelling incident, and the missing body camera chargers / low battery equipment issues.
