Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, theft response, suspicious behaviour checks, public nuisance response, vagrant move-ons, Police follow-up, and equipment issues.
Morning shift covered Echo, Alpha, and Bravo Routes across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Commercial Bay, Kiwibank, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, Victoria Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Customs Street, Metro Woolworths, Aotea Gifts, and surrounding retail areas. Morning incidents included a male sleeping inside McDonald’s Britomart, vagrant belongings outside Unichem Pharmacy, a management report about monks allegedly scamming people, a PB Tech theft report, a Police follow-up regarding a previous Chancery Square incident, a female sitting outside 229 Queen Street, and reports of a male yelling near 95 Queen Street.
Team 2 operated from 09:00 to 17:00, covering Queen Street, Aotea Square, Elliott Street, Victoria Street, Federal Street, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, PB Tech, Commercial Bay, Mountain Warehouse, 95 Queen Street, and Cotton On. Team 2 attended PB Tech for a theft report, searched for the POI around lower Queen Street, attended Mountain Warehouse for a suspicious individual where a bag search found no items, moved along a vagrant near 95 Queen Street, and attempted to intercept a Cotton On theft suspect running towards Aotea Square. The suspect was not located.
Afternoon shift completed Alpha, Echo, and Beta Routes. Guards responded to reports of rowdy individuals near Victoria Street West / ANZ, a repeat offender at Pop Mart who paid for previously stolen items, a Cotton On theft, and a male begging outside Woolworths. Patrols also covered Commercial Bay, Queens Arcade, Shortland Street, Darby Street, Victoria Street, Chancery Square, Freyberg Square, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, Aotea Square, Father Ted’s, and Elliott Stables.
Night shift completed Delta and Echo Routes across Britomart, Commercial Bay, Upper Queen Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Victoria Street, Albert Street, Customs Street, Fort Street, High Street, Chancery Square, Freyberg Square, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Federal Street, and Wyndham Street. The main night response involved McDonald’s Queen Street, where a group of vagrants sleeping outside were moved along and two arguing vagrants were calmed and moved on.
Overall, the key matters were the PB Tech theft, Mountain Warehouse suspicious individual / bag search, Cotton On theft and failed interception, Pop Mart repeat offender payment resolution, 95 Queen Street public peace concern, Unichem Pharmacy vagrant obstruction, and the missing body camera chargers.
