Serious / Notable Findings
Red Radisson assault attempt / Police attendance
At 22:02, guards responded to Red Radisson regarding a male individual they wanted removed. The individual initially complied and left, but at 22:10 crossed to the Lorne Street fountains area and attempted to physically assault a male. Guards intervened and Police were called. Police arrived at 22:31 and dealt with the individual and victim. Guard two remained as witness for statement.
Known aggressive male at Lovisa
At 08:44, Lovisa reported a male street vagrant sitting in front of their glass window. Guards attended at 08:54 and identified the male as Junior, a known aggressive individual who had previously attacked one of the guards and had recently been released. Bodycam footage was captured.
Aggressive male with walking stick at McDonald’s Queen Street
At 12:52, management reported an aggressive male with a walking stick waving it aggressively at members of the public at McDonald’s Queen Street. Guards attended at 13:00 and checked the area and premises. No active issue was located on arrival.
Two young boys causing public damage / disturbance
At 10:43, Community Patrol reported two young boys causing a disturbance and smashing items in public spaces. Last seen heading towards Britomart. Second team was notified and attended the area of interest.
Disorderly vagrants reported by Puff and Kiwi
At 13:42, Puff and Kiwi reported two street vagrants arguing, yelling, and using profanity. Guards attended and checked the area, but the individuals were not located.
Metropolis / Deng Deng public nuisance incident
At 00:35, Metropolis reported two males drinking at Deng Deng Restaurant and using the side of the motel as a toilet. They initially refused to comply and swore at the guard. Guards attended and the males complied without further issue.
The Fiddler intoxicated male refusal to leave
At 23:01, The Fiddler Irish Bar requested assistance with an intoxicated male refusing to leave. Guards attended, spoke with the POI, and he complied and left.
Ops phone battery issues
Ops phone battery was critically low multiple times during the day, including 8% at morning deployment, 11% at afternoon deployment, 5% at 19:30, and 10% during night shift. This required repeated standby/charging periods.
Equipment issue
Two camera chargers were missing at handover and should be followed up.

Summary
The shift involved routine patrols, welfare checks, response calls, public reassurance, vagrant move-ons, alcohol-related behaviour, suspicious/disorderly activity, Police attendance, and equipment/battery issues.
Morning shift covered Echo Route and completed welfare checks across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Wakefield Street, Lorne Street, High Street, Victoria Street, Shortland Street, Pocket Park, Fort Street, Chancery Square, Freyberg Place, and surrounding areas. The main morning concerns were the known aggressive male at Lovisa, reports of two young boys damaging public spaces, an aggressive male with a walking stick at McDonald’s Queen Street, and two vagrants arguing near Queen Street.
Afternoon shift completed Alpha and Echo Routes, including welfare checks across upper and lower Queen Street, Aotea Square, Skyworld, Commercial Bay, Vulcan Lane, Shortland Street, Queens Arcade, Chancery Square, Freyberg Square, and surrounding businesses. The most serious afternoon/evening matter occurred after the Red Radisson response call, where the male individual left the premises but then attempted to physically assault another male near the Lorne Street fountains. Guards intervened and Police attended.
Night shift responded to The Fiddler Irish Bar for an intoxicated male refusing to leave and to Metropolis/Deng Deng Restaurant regarding two males drinking and using the side of the motel as a toilet. Both incidents were resolved without further escalation. Night patrols across Beta, Delta, and Echo Routes were completed, with all checks reported clear.
Overall, the key serious matters were the Red Radisson assault attempt requiring Police attendance, the known aggressive male Junior at Lovisa, the aggressive male with walking stick at McDonald’s Queen Street, and the Metropolis/Deng Deng public nuisance incident.
