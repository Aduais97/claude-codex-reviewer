Serious / Notable Findings
Nike theft recovery
At 15:13, Nike reported a male had stolen from the store and provided a photo description. Guards located the male at McDonald’s Queen Street at 15:14. The male complied and returned the stolen goods. Items were returned to Nike at 15:16. Auror report to be completed.
102 Albert Street trespass
At 21:15, guards responded to 102 Albert Street regarding a male refusing to leave after breaching building policy. Guards attended, spoke with the male, and issued him a trespass notice on behalf of the premises. Bodycam footage captured and Auror report filed.
Male vagrant at Celine’s / Darby Street
At 16:55, guards observed a male vagrant sitting outside Celine’s under the influence of legal substances. Guards asked him to move along and he complied. Auror report completed.
McDonald’s Queen Street smoking incident
At 03:24, guards responded to McDonald’s Queen Street regarding a male smoking inside the premises. Guards attended and moved the male on without further issue.
Follow-up from previous serious incidents
Morning shift completed Auror reports regarding the male defecating behind 220 Queen Street and the aggressive male involved in the Glassons incident. Glassons staff also thanked guards for assistance during the previous day’s incident.

Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, visible security presence, business engagement, incident reporting, theft recovery, trespass action, and response to antisocial behaviour.
Morning shift completed Alpha, Echo, and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Queens Wharf, Customs Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral precinct, High Street, Freyberg Place, Women’s Memorial Hall, Chancery Square, O’Connell Lane, Vulcan Lane, Fort Street, Auckland Harbour Suites, Queens Arcade, Aotea Square, Lorne Street, Wellesley Street, and surrounding businesses. No major incidents occurred during morning patrol. The team also completed Auror reports relating to previous incidents at 220 Queen Street and Glassons.
Afternoon shift responded to Nike Queen Street for a theft incident. Guards located the male at McDonald’s Queen Street, recovered the stolen items, and returned them to Nike. Guards also moved on a male vagrant from outside Celine’s, completed welfare checks across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Victoria Street, Lorne Street, Commercial Bay, Gore Street, Fort Street, and Freyberg / Chancery Square. Later, guards responded to 102 Albert Street and issued a trespass notice to a male who refused to leave after breaching building policy.
Night shift completed Alpha and Beta Route patrols across Chemist Warehouse / Kathmandu, Vulcan Lane, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Pita Pit, Ace Karaoke, Aotea Square, Bar101, McDonald’s Queen Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Gore Street, St Patrick’s Square, Victoria Street, Lorne Street, and Wakefield Street. The main night incident was a male smoking inside McDonald’s Queen Street, who was moved on without escalation.
Overall, the key matters were the Nike theft recovery, the 102 Albert Street trespass notice, the Celine’s vagrant move-on, and the McDonald’s Queen Street smoking incident.
