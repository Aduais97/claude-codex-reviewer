Serious / Notable Findings
Glassons major disorder incident / Police arrest
At 13:08, Glassons reported a male causing a major disturbance inside the store. The POI was throwing clothing outside, slamming mannequins and shelves to the ground, and verbally abusing staff. Guards arrived at 13:13 and found the male still actively behaving aggressively. Police attended at 13:20, arrested the POI, and placed him in the Police vehicle. Guards advised staff not to clean or touch the scene so Police could take evidence photos.
Physical altercation outside Bar101
At 11:11, guards witnessed three street vagrants involved in a physical altercation outside Bar101. Guards intervened, separated the parties, de-escalated the incident, and moved them on in different directions.
Threat made towards guard near Wellesley Street
At 20:10, guards observed a male yelling at members of the public near Wellesley Street. When guards approached and attempted to calm him, the male became offended and threatened to assault a guard. Guards instructed him to move along and maintained presence in the area.
POI defecating behind 220 Building / Lorne Street
At 11:00, guards located the POI known for defecating behind the 220 Building on Lorne Street. Guards warned the individual that Police would be involved if he returned and continued the behaviour. Video footage is available.
Repeated vagrant sleeping incidents at McDonald’s Queen Street
McDonald’s Queen Street required assistance multiple times for individuals sleeping inside the premises. Guards attended at 08:41, 19:50, and 02:20. Each person was woken and moved on without escalation.
Police activity outside McDonald’s Queen Street
At 17:22, Police were seen speaking with a male vagrant outside McDonald’s Queen Street. Guards stood by in case assistance was required.
Ops phone battery issue
Ops phone battery was critically low at the start of morning shift and required standby charging before full patrols could continue. It also required charging during afternoon shift.

Summary
The shift involved routine patrols, business welfare checks, public reassurance, response calls, vagrant move-ons, physical altercation de-escalation, a major retail disorder incident, and Police attendance.
Morning shift began with the Ops phone critically low at 6%, requiring patrols to remain on standby while the phone charged. Once patrols resumed, guards completed checks across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Wakefield Street, Lorne Street, High Street, Victoria Street, Shortland Street, Pocket Park, Fort Street, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, and surrounding business areas. The key morning incidents were a male vagrant sleeping inside McDonald’s Queen Street, the POI located for defecating behind the 220 Building, a physical altercation between three vagrants outside Bar101, and the major Glassons disorder incident that resulted in Police arrest.
Afternoon shift completed patrols and welfare checks across upper Queen Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Victoria Street, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, Albert Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, and Gore Street. Main afternoon concerns included Police speaking with a vagrant outside McDonald’s Queen Street, a male sleeping inside McDonald’s Queen Street, and a male near Wellesley Street yelling at the public and threatening to assault a guard.
Night shift completed Alpha, Beta, Delta, Echo, and Charlie Routes, covering Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Britomart, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Victoria Street, High Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Auckland Harbour Suites, Wyndham Street, Hobson Street, and Lorne Street. The main night response involved a female vagrant sleeping in the McDonald’s Queen Street lobby, who was moved on without issue.
Overall, the main serious matters were the Glassons major disorder incident resulting in Police arrest, the physical altercation outside Bar101, the threat made towards a guard near Wellesley Street, and the repeated vagrant sleeping incidents at McDonald’s Queen Street.
