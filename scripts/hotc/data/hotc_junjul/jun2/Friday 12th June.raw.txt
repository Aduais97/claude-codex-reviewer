Serious / Notable Findings
Mirrou attempted theft / assault / verbal trespass
At 16:38, guards responded to Mirrou regarding attempted theft. At 16:48, the situation escalated when the POI refused a bag search and an altercation occurred between the POI and the manager. HOTC intervened using reasonable force to separate the parties and escorted the POI out. Police were contacted, and a verbal trespass was issued for attempted theft and assault. Police were later provided footage and information.
McDonald’s Queen Street bathroom / missing belongings incident
At 11:52, McDonald’s Queen Street requested assistance for a male who had been in the bathroom too long. The male became upset after discovering his belongings had been removed from the front of the store. CCTV review showed construction workers had placed his items in a rubbish bin while cleaning. Guards calmed the situation, and the male apologised before leaving.
The Coffee Club Wyndham Street sleeping POI
At 12:25, guards responded to a male sleeping inside The Coffee Club and refusing to leave. Guards woke him, explained customer-only use, and moved him on without escalation.
McDonald’s Britomart aggressive female blocking entry
At 21:30, guards responded to McDonald’s Britomart regarding a female blocking public entry and acting aggressively. The female left before guard arrival.
Synthetic drug medical incident observed
At 21:43, guards encountered Council guards and two ambulances attending to a vagrant requiring medical assistance after consuming synthetic drugs. Guards stood by in case further assistance was needed.
Coco Lane lost phones returned
Two phones were handed to HOTC by Coco Lane and later successfully returned to the female owner at 22:07.
McDonald’s Queen Street repeated vagrant issues
McDonald’s Queen Street required assistance during morning and night shift for vagrants sleeping near or inside the premises. Guards attended and moved individuals along where required.

Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, response calls, lost property management, vagrant move-ons, public assistance, a medical-related observation, and one serious attempted theft / assault incident.
Morning shift completed Alpha and Echo Routes across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Elliott Street, Atrium on Elliott, Victoria Street, Kingston Street, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral precinct, Commercial Bay, Queens Wharf, Queens Arcade, Kiwibank, Gore Street, Wilson Carpark, Freyberg Place, Women’s Memorial Hall, Chancery Square, Lorne Street, Auckland Library, Mayoral Drive, and surrounding areas. Main morning incidents involved McDonald’s Queen Street, where a street vagrant was sleeping outside before an audit, and later a male became upset about missing belongings after being in the bathroom too long. Guards reviewed CCTV, established what happened, calmed the situation, and moved the male along. Guards also moved a male sleeping inside The Coffee Club Wyndham Street.
Afternoon shift responded to a suspicious person at Cotton On, collected two lost phones from Coco Lane, and dealt with a serious attempted theft at Mirrou Queen Street. The Mirrou incident escalated into an altercation between the POI and store manager. HOTC separated the parties, escorted the POI out, contacted Police, issued a verbal trespass, and later provided footage and information to Police. Guards also attended McDonald’s Queen Street, Victoria Street Convenience Store, McDonald’s Britomart, and assisted with a medical-related situation involving a vagrant who had reportedly consumed synthetic drugs. The two lost phones were later returned to the owner.
Night shift completed welfare checks across Bar101, Queen Street, McDonald’s Britomart, Reign & Pour, Ace Nightclub, 77 Convenience Store, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, Lorne Street, Wakefield Street, and McDonald’s Queen Street. The main night incident involved two vagrants sleeping inside McDonald’s Queen Street and refusing to leave. Guards attended, moved them on, and maintained presence until morning shift handover.
Overall, the main serious matter was the Mirrou attempted theft / assault / verbal trespass incident, followed by the McDonald’s Queen Street missing belongings dispute, the synthetic drug medical assistance observation, and the repeated vagrant-related issues at McDonald’s Queen Street.
