Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, overtime handover support, response calls, public reassurance, de-escalation, alcohol-related interventions, and public safety monitoring across the HOTC area. Morning shift completed patrols across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Wellesley Street, Federal Street, Sky Tower area, St Patrick’s Cathedral Church, Hobson Street, Wyndham Street, Albert Street, Commercial Bay, TK Square, Lorne Street, High Street, Vulcan Lane, Fort Lane, Gore Street, Wilson Carpark, Chancery Square, and surrounding areas.
Morning shift responded to McDonald’s Britomart regarding a vagrant sleeping outside on a public bench and later a separate call for a vagrant sleeping inside the restaurant. Guards also intervened in an argument involving a group of four vagrants at Hobson Street and Wyndham Street, de-escalating the situation and walking with the group until they separated. Vagrants drinking at Vulcan Lane were also instructed to close their alcohol containers, and they complied.
Afternoon patrols covered Queen Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Customs Street, Gore Street, Fort Lane, High Street, Chancery Square, Vulcan Lane, Aotea Square, Elliott Street, Darby Street, Victoria Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, and surrounding areas. A call from McDonald’s Britomart regarding teenagers on bikes outside the restaurant was monitored, with no further issue found.
Night shift responded to several incidents, including a loud vagrant throwing items on High Street, a report of a male asking uncomfortable questions to a female worker at Snickel Lane, a group harassing a security guard at Kemuri, a difficult male at Q Convenience Store, and a potential brawl near old Smith & Caughey’s. Guards separated parties, monitored the victim group, ensured they safely reached transport, and removed alcohol from juveniles/minors during the shift. Police presence was observed in the area, but no direct Police handover was recorded.
