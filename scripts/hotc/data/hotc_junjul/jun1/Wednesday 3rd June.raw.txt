Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, rain-adjusted visibility patrols, business reassurance, response calls, and follow-up intelligence gathering across the HOTC area. Morning shift completed Alpha, Charlie, and Echo patrols across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Victoria Street, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Federal Square, Chancery Square, High Street, Vulcan Lane, Fort Street, Queens Arcade, Imperial Lane, and surrounding areas. Due to rain, guards maintained presence in sheltered areas where possible.
Morning shift responded to Localito Convenience Store regarding a male individual harassing customers and yelling at members of the public. Guards moved him away from the store, and Auckland Council later attended with Police dispatch. Guards also responded to a female individual passed out in front of Michael Hill, but she had moved along before arrival.
Afternoon shift completed welfare checks and patrols across Queen Street, Chemist Warehouse, Imperial Lane, Commercial Bay, High Street, Fort Street, Gore Street, Aotea Square, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Victoria Street, Albert Street, and surrounding areas. Several stores provided intelligence on prior thefts and persons of interest, including OK Gift Shop, Look Sharp, Brand Warehouse, Coco Lane, and Tatty’s. Guards also responded to 220 Queen Street to assist the Data Centre with heavy lifting. Later, guards responded to Puffing Kiwi regarding an intoxicated male yelling and swearing at members of the public. The individual was moved along without further issue.
Night shift completed checks across Chemist Warehouse, Kathmandu, Vulcan Lane, McDonald’s Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Mart, Ace Karaoke, 77 Convenience Store, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, Bar101, McDonald’s Queen Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Gore Street, St Patrick’s Square, Aotea Gifts, Lorne Street, Wyndham Street, Durham Street, and upper Queen Street. No major incidents or escalations were reported during night shift.
