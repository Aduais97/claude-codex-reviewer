Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, response calls, theft prevention, public assistance, alcohol removal, and public safety support across the HOTC area. Morning shift completed Charlie and Alpha Routes across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Wakefield Street, Lorne Street, High Street, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Federal Street, Victoria Street, and surrounding retail areas.
Morning shift responded to Lumi Cafe regarding street vagrants sleeping near the store and moved one group along. A later Lumi Cafe call involved two vagrants sleeping on a public bench, and staff were advised that guards could not move them because they were not on private property. Guards also responded to Glassons regarding Chuma Cheme, who was acting aggressively and had store items. The items were recovered without issue and Chuma left the store. Nike later reported a separate theft by a female wearing black leggings and a brown jacket, with photo evidence to be attached.
Afternoon shift completed welfare checks across Queen Street, Commercial Bay, Gore Street, Fort Street, High Street, Chancery Square, Lorne Street, and Bar101. Guards also completed a building sweep at 220 Queen Street after the Nike POI was seen entering the building, but she was not located. Later, guards removed alcohol from two male individuals and assisted a member of the public blocking the road near Hazy Tiger.
Night shift maintained presence around Bar101, Sapphire, Britomart, Commercial Bay, and McDonald’s Queen Street. Guards assisted a member of the public who reported being threatened by a group of minors and later escorted him safely to his ride home. Another member of the public was assisted with directions. A response call from McDonald’s Queen Street involved a vagrant disturbing customers and wanting to fight, but the individual left before guard arrival.
