Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, response calls, theft prevention, recovered property, verbal trespass action, and weather-adjusted patrols across the HOTC area. Morning shift completed Alpha, Delta, and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Durham Street, Wyndham Street, Shortland Street, Pocket Park, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Federal Square, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, Wakefield Street, Lorne Street, High Street, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Kiwibank, Woolworths, Chemist Warehouse, Crowne Plaza, and Atrium on Elliott. No major incidents were reported during morning shift, apart from a management assistance request at headquarters.
Afternoon shift responded to several business calls. Guards attended Doshan at 131 Queen Street for a male vagrant sitting outside the store, who was moved along without issue. Guards also responded to Hallensteins Queen Street for a suspicious male who attempted to walk out wearing unpaid items; guards stopped him, and he complied by removing the items. Tatty’s Designer Recycle reported a theft and later located the female POI on High Street. Guards located her near Vulcan Lane, but no stolen items were found. She was issued a verbal trespass from Tatty’s. Cotton On also reported a female POI who had stolen from the store. The evening shift team located her on a bus, stopped the bus, and recovered the stolen goods, which were returned to Cotton On.
Night shift completed patrols and welfare checks across Chemist Warehouse, Kathmandu, Vulcan Lane, McDonald’s Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, McDonald’s Queen Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Gore Street, St Patrick’s Cathedral Church, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Durham Street, and Wyndham Street. Streets were quiet overnight, and due to wet and slippery surfaces, patrols were completed with caution. No major incidents or escalations were reported during night shift.
