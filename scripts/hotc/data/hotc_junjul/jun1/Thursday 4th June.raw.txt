Summary
The day involved routine patrols, welfare checks, business reassurance, theft reporting, disturbance response, Team 2 support, and one night-shift response call involving a trespassed individual at McDonald’s Queen Street.
Morning shift completed Alpha and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Victoria Street, Durham Street, Wyndham Street, Shortland Street, Imperial Lane, Commercial Bay, TK Square, Albert Street, Federal Square, Cathedral Church, High Street, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Queens Wharf, Queens Arcade, and surrounding retail areas. Morning shift responded to Mirrou after staff reported a female individual and her partner had committed theft, allegedly taking two skirts and one shirt. Guards attended, obtained a photo description, and monitored the area. The second HOTC team was also sent to 27–30 Victoria Street regarding a street vagrant obstructing the building entrance. Later, Roma Blooms reported a male causing a disturbance at the top of Victoria Street. Guards attended, but the male had already left after urinating beside the store.
Team 2 covered the upper CBD and supported multiple areas throughout the day, including Queen Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Victoria Street, Auckland Library, Lorne Street, Queens Court, and 27 Victoria Street West. Team 2 dealt with an aggressive male vagrant at top of Victoria Street who picked up a chair and later moved towards Hobson Street, a male vagrant disturbing World Vision volunteer workers, and a female vagrant obstructing the entrance at 27 Victoria Street West. Team 2 also gathered information from Lorne Street businesses regarding a repeat POI from a previous Tsujiri / Bao & Noodles / Meat Fresh incident involving damage to store displays.
Afternoon shift responded to a concern from Dangerfield regarding a male preaching and being pushy towards customers and staff. Guards located the male near McDonald’s Queen Street, spoke with him, and advised him not to continue preaching in or directly outside stores. The remainder of the afternoon patrols were completed without further issues.
Night shift completed Beta, Delta, and Echo Routes across Queen Street, Victoria Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, O’Connell Street, High Street, Fort Street, Gore Street, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Aotea Square, Mayoral Drive, Customs Street, Darby Street, Elliott Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, and surrounding areas. A response call was received from McDonald’s Queen Street regarding a trespassed male vagrant sleeping inside the lobby. Guards attended, woke the individual, reminded him he was trespassed, and moved him along without further issue.
