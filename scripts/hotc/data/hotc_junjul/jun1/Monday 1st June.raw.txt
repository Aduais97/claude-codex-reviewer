Summary
The day involved routine patrols, public holiday welfare checks, response calls, public reassurance, and weather-adjusted patrols across the HOTC area. Due to the public holiday, several stores opened later or had reduced trading hours. Morning shift completed Alpha, Echo, and Charlie Routes across Queen Street, Wyndham Street, Shortland Street, Pocket Park, Albert Street, Federal Square, Commercial Bay, Britomart, Gore Street, Fort Street, Lower High Street, Vulcan Lane, Freyberg Place, Chancery Square, Lorne Street, Aotea Square, Victoria Street, and surrounding areas.
Morning shift responded to McDonald’s Queen Street regarding a male sleeping inside the lobby and refusing to leave. The POI was woken and moved along without issue. Guards also responded to The Coffee Club, where a female street vagrant had been blocking the entrance but left before arrival. A further response call was received from McDonald’s Britomart regarding a male sleeping inside; he left before guards arrived. Guards then responded to Cotton On for a trespassed female individual known to be aggressive and non-compliant. Police were called, and the POI left before Police engaged with her nearby. Guards also attended Pizza Amour, where a male sleeping at the doorway was moved along without issue.
Afternoon shift completed Alpha and Echo Routes, with welfare checks across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Vulcan Lane, Durham Street, Shortland Street, Commercial Bay, Darby Street, Victoria Street, Federal Street, Chancery Square, Freyberg Square, Queens Arcade, and Ace Karaoke. Heavy rain affected the patrol pattern, so guards continued checks at a slower pace while using sheltered areas to maintain visible presence. No major incidents were reported during afternoon shift.
Night shift completed patrols and checks across Chemist Warehouse, Kathmandu, Vulcan Lane, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Albert Street, Victoria Street, Aotea Square, McDonald’s Queen Street, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, St Patrick’s Cathedral Church, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Wilson Carpark, Durham Street, and Wyndham Street. Streets were quiet, and due to wet/slippery surfaces, patrols were completed with caution. No major incidents or escalations were reported during night shift.
