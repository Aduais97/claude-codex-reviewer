Serious / Notable Findings
Possible break-in at Pita Pit Cafe, Wyndham Street
At 21:28, guards discovered a possible break-in at Pita Pit Cafe, with the door damaged. Management was contacted and the incident was reported to 105 at 21:45. Night shift was advised to carry out regular checks of the location to deter further activity.
Ace Karaoke incident involving possible MC gang members
At 00:56, guards received a call from Ace Karaoke security requesting assistance. Guards arrived at 02:03 and were advised of a male being a nuisance towards guards, while a large group inside one room claimed to be part of an MC gang. The group left aggressively due to the amount owed for the room and their attempt to take alcohol out of the venue. Guards maintained security presence after the group left.
Cotton On theft incident
At 16:28, guards responded to Cotton On regarding theft. A vagrant was stopped and the merchandise was returned to the store. No further issues reported.
Backpackers Albert Street aggressive vagrant / trespass request
At 21:02, Backpackers Albert Street requested assistance with an aggressive vagrant. The vagrant left before guards arrived, but staff requested trespass action and provided POI details. Guards kept watch for the individual.
McDonald’s Queen Street trespassed POI
At 00:32, guards responded to McDonald’s Queen Street. The POI had been trespassed and was causing trouble with a tourist. Video provided.
Vagrant consuming alcohol and disturbing public peace
At 17:45, a vagrant was observed consuming alcohol in public and refused to move along when asked. Guards later managed to get the individual onto a bus and leave the area.
Suspicious male at Red Radisson
At 13:18, Red Radisson reported a male showing suspicious behaviour. Guards attended and received a photo of the individual.

Summary
The report covered routine patrols, business welfare checks, response calls, theft recovery, vagrant move-ons, public alcohol concerns, suspicious behaviour monitoring, a possible break-in, and venue support.
Morning shift completed patrols across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Darby Street, Wellesley Street, Federal Street, Wyndham Street, Vulcan Lane, O’Connell Street, Chancery Square, Britomart, Commercial Bay, Gore Street, Fort Street, Albert Street, and Victoria Street. Morning incidents included a vagrant encampment argument near Wyndham Street and Albert Street, a Woolworths Victoria Street vagrant complaint, and a suspicious male report at Red Radisson.
Afternoon shift responded to theft at Cotton On, where the merchandise was recovered and returned. Guards also moved a vagrant from Starbucks, monitored a POI consuming alcohol in public, and completed welfare checks across High Street, Chancery Square, Fort Street, Gore Street, Auckland Harbour Suites, and AKA Red Radisson. The most serious afternoon/evening matter was the possible break-in at Pita Pit Cafe on Wyndham Street, which was reported to 105.
Night shift completed checks across Queen Street, Aotea Square, Lorne Street, Elliott Street, Darby Street, Commercial Bay, Fort Street, Wilson Carpark, Freyberg Square, Chancery Square, Kitchener Street, St Patrick’s Square, Wyndham Street, and Durham Street. Night shift responded to McDonald’s Queen Street regarding a trespassed POI causing trouble with a tourist, and to Ace Karaoke regarding nuisance behaviour and a group claiming MC gang affiliation.
Overall, the main serious matters were the possible break-in at Pita Pit Cafe, the Ace Karaoke incident involving possible MC gang members, the Cotton On theft recovery, and the McDonald’s Queen Street trespassed POI incident.
